ES5000 Graduate English I 
 
 
PRESENTATION 
 
Instructions 
 
Prepare and deliver a 4- to 5-minute presentation introducing yourself and your area of research. 
Please use visual aids to illustrate your words. The following are points you could include in your 
presentation: 
1) Introduce yourself and something interesting about your background (e.g., family, 
hometown, country). 
2) Describe what excites you about your area of study and/or research focus. 
3) Explain why you chose to study this topic at NUS. 
4) Discuss what you hope to do once you complete your NUS degree. 
 
The following criteria will be used to assess your presentation: 
 
Content and Organization 30% 
- Points well suited to the audience 
- Technical concepts are easy to understand 
- Appropriate organizational format (e.g., past, present, future; problem/solution; goals, 
procedures, outcomes) 
- Clear transitions between points 
 
Language and Vocal Elements 30% 
- Grammatical accuracy 
- Appropriate word choice 
- Pronunciation 
- Volume and vocal energy 
- Pacing, fluency & pauses 
 
Visual Elements 30% 
- Eye contact 
- Facial expression 
- Gesture 
- Posture & stance 
- Confidence & enthusiasm 
 
Visual Aids 10% 
- Design of visual aids 
- Use of visual aids 
 
Please practice your presentation so that you speak for a minimum of four and no more than five 
minutes.
