little 'Dash' seemed to feel the emotion of the moment, and
cuddled up to his Chinese friend with an exceptional dis-
play of affection. Honest Badruddin Khan, too, had tears
in his eyes when we parted. Then, as I rode on, the
quivering glare and heat of the desert seemed to descend
like a luminous curtain and to hide from me the most
cherished aspects of my Turkestan life.
