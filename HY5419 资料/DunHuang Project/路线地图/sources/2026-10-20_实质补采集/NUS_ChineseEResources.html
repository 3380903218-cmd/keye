Introduction

This subject guide covers resources available at the NUS Libraries. This is not a comprehensive subject guide, but rather a selective list of materials that are most useful for locating information in Chinese E-Resources.

    Digital Gems is a gateway to rare, historical, and primary source materials from NUS Libraries Special Collection in digital format.
