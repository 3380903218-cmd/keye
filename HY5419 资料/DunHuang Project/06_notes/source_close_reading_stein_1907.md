# 来源措辞近读：Stein 1907（S07）

Stein’s account describes an “agreement arrived at” for fifty Chinese and five Tibetan bundles, records that the payment amounted to “four horseshoes of silver,” and says that two midnight trips allowed the loads to be “safely removed” to his storeroom (Serindia, vol. 2, pp. 824-825). He later frames the action as letting him “rescue for Western scholarship” the relics.

For the data model, these phrases are not interchangeable. `acquire` and `agreement` describe the transaction as Stein presents it; `secret removal` describes the physical process; `rescue for Western scholarship` is a retrospective justification. The pilot therefore keeps the original wording in `SourceWording` and uses the more neutral controlled values `acquisition` and `physical_transfer` in `Relationship`.

Limit: this is one explorer’s account, with self-justifying language and approximate quantities. It supports event coding but cannot by itself establish legal ownership, consent, or moral responsibility.
