## Field footage and production

Field footage was supplied by Wang Xiwen. Thirteen original clips are available; eleven are used in this cut, with selected views returning at later points. The exact filming dates and every street location have not been verified. People’s residency, visitor status, business costs or length of residence are not inferred from their appearance.

English narration uses the local macOS Daniel voice, split into 19 replaceable WAV blocks. English captions are based on the script and selected original statements, with sentence-based timing. AI assisted the English script, graphics and edit preparation. No resident interview or district-wide survey has been fabricated. The initial cut remains subject to the author’s content and pronunciation review.
