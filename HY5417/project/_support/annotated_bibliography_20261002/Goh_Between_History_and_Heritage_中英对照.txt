Between History and Heritage: Post-Colonialism, Globalisation, and the 
Remaking of Malacca, Penang, and Singapore
历史与遗产之间：后殖民主义、全球化与马六甲、槟城和新加坡的重塑
Daniel P.S. Goh
Department of Sociology, National University of Singapore; dsong@nus.edu.sg
新加坡国立大学社会学系；dsong@nus.edu.sg
Abstract
摘要
The three cities of Malacca, Penang and Singapore share a century-long history as the British Straits Settlements, with  
similar multicultural traditions and urban morphology of dense shophouse districts. In the post-colonial period, these  
have been the basis for the production of heritage for urban renewal, civic identity formation, and international  
tourism. Yet, each city has approached the production of its history as heritage in different ways. The differences have  
been specified in terms of whether heritage production has been led by the state, market or civil society, and criticised  
as ideology or ambivalently interpreted as formative of identity in the face of globalisation. As colonial port-cities  
integrating into or becoming a new nation-state, I argue that the production of heritage in the three cities is driven by  
the politics of post-colonial identity interacting with the political economy of urban redevelopment. I argue that the  
production of heritage is one facet in the production of space and an increasingly important one in globalising Asian  
urbanisms. We can specify the differences in production of heritage space in the three cities in terms of the orientation  
of imagination and the ends of production. I show that the three city-states have been interpreting its history for  
heritage production in either Asian or cosmopolitan imaginations and configuring its heritage production for either  
political identity formation or economic product development, or a mix of both. The differences, I demonstrate, are  
caused by the differing politics of post-colonial identity and economic development involving the three cities.

INTRODUCTION
引言
FOR OVER A CENTURY from 1826 to 1946, the three port-cities of Malacca, Penang and Singapore shared a common  
history as the Straits Settlements under British rule. Malacca and Penang were separated from Singapore in 1946 and  
eventually incorporated into the independent Federation of Malaya in 1957. Singapore belatedly joined the Federation,  
renamed Malaysia, along with the former British Borneo states of Sarawak and Sabah in 1963, but separated and became  
independent in 1965.

从 1826 年至 1946 年的一个多世纪里，马六甲、槟城和新加坡这三座港口城市在英国统治下共同构成海峡殖民地。
1946 年，马六甲和槟城与新加坡分离，并最终于 1957 年并入独立的马来亚联合邦。新加坡较晚于 1963 年同前英
国北婆罗洲的砂拉越和沙巴一起加入该联邦，当时联邦已更名为马来西亚；但新加坡随后于 1965 年退出，并成为
独立国家。
Through the 1970s and 1980s, Penang and Singapore both industrialised. The islands became heavily urbanised. In the  
1990s  and  2000s,  Singapore  instituted  economic  reforms  to  develop  into  a  post-industrial  financial  hub.  Urban  
redevelopment plans that adapted historical precincts in the city centre to new economic uses played a large part in  
transforming it into a global city. During this period, George Town, Penang’s inner city centre, went into a slow decline,  
only recently reviving its economic fortunes and experiencing urban renewal after obtaining the UNESCO World Heritage  
City status. Malacca shared the World Heritage City status with Penang, which helped boost the developing tourist industry  
in the old trading town.
在 20 世纪 70 和 80 年代，槟城和新加坡都经历了工业化，两座岛屿也迅速城市化。进入 20 世纪 90 年代和 21 世纪
初，新加坡推行经济改革，发展为后工业金融中心。将市中心历史街区改造以适应新的经济用途的城市再开发规
划，在新加坡转型为全球城市的过 程中发挥了重要作用。与 此同时，槟城内城乔治市经历了缓慢衰退，直到最近
获得联合国教科文组织世界遗产城市地 位后，经济才重新复苏，并开始出现城市更新。马六甲与槟城共同 获得世
界遗产城市地位，这也推动了这座古老贸易城市不断发展的旅游业。
The shared 120-year history created similarities in cultural traditions and urban morphology in the three cities, such as  
hybridised Chinese, Indian and Eurasian festivals, and dense colonial shophouse districts unique to coastal cities in the  
region.  These  shared  traditions  have  been  the  basis  for  the  production  of  heritage  for  urban  renewal,  civic  identity  
formation, and international tourism. Yet, each city has approached the production of its history as heritage in very different  
ways. How do we specify the differences in the production of heritage between the three cities? How can we explain the  
differences?

Given the geopolitical and economic legacies as colonial port-cities integrating into the new nation-state of Malaysia, or in  
the case of Singapore, becoming a nation-state, I argue that the production of heritage in the three cities is driven by the  
politics  of  post-colonial  identity  interacting  with  the  political  economy  of  urban  redevelopment  in  the  context  of  
globalisation. I begin first by discussing two theoretical approaches to the production of history as heritage and evaluate  
current studies of heritage and urban redevelopment in the three cities. I draw on Henri Lefebvre (1991) to make a critique  
of the two approaches and argue that the production of heritage is one facet in the production of space and an increasingly  
important one in globalising Asian urbanisms.
鉴于这些城市曾作为殖民港口城市，并在地缘政治与经济上留下了深刻遗产——马六甲和槟城被纳入新建立的马来
西亚民族国家，而新加坡则自身成为一个民族国家——我认为，在全球化背景下，三座城市的遗产生产是由后殖民
身份政治与城市再开发的政治经济相互作用所推动的。本文 首先讨论将历史生产为遗产的两 种理论路径，并评估

两种路径，并主张遗产生产是空间生产的一个方面，而且在不断全球化的亚洲城市发展中正变得日益重要。
We can specify the differences in production of heritage space in the three cities in terms of the orientation of imagination  
and the ends of production. In the remaining sections, I show that the three city-states have been interpreting its history for

caused by the differing politics of post-colonial identity and economic development involving the three cities.

THE PRODUCTION OF HERITAGE SPACE
遗产空间的生产
Discussions of the politics of heritage can be divided into two approaches. The critical approach emphasises two things.  
First, heritage is essentially a commodity produced for a tourist or domestic cultural market. Second, in its production,  
heritage also reflects the dominant culture and ideology on the part of the ruling elites. For Hewison (1989), the production  
of heritage fits into the cultural logic of late capitalism as a spectacular, hyper-real commodity that ransacks historical  
materials for superficial consumption in the present. Harrison (2005) argues that heritage is closely related to market  
ideologies and the power to impose one’s view of the past. Many scholars see Singapore as a prime example of the  
hegemonic production of heritage for the market. Since the early 1990s, the state has actively sought to promote heritage as  
a “tourism commodity” (Chang 1997) by carving out the historic city centre into distinct multiracial zones, Chinatown,  
Kampong Glam and Little India, surrounding the civic centre of British colonial buildings. These ethnic neighbourhoods  
have been preserved in the Chinese-Malay-Indian-Others racial grid of official multiracialism. As a result, they have  
become ethnic-themed precincts functioning as tourist attractions in the state’s global city making plans (Chang 2000; Yeoh  
2005).
关于遗产政治的 讨论可以分为两种路径。批判路径 强调两点。第一，遗产本质上是一种面向旅游市场或国 内文化
市场生产的商品。第二，在遗产生产过程中，它也反映了统治精英的主导文化与意识形态。对 Hewison（1989）而
言，遗产生产符合晚期资本主义的文化逻辑：它成为一种壮观的、超真实的商品，掠取历史材料，供当下进行表
层消费。Harrison（2005）认为，遗产与市场意识形态以及将某种过去观强加于人的权力密切相关。许多学者将新
加坡视为为市场进行霸权式遗产生产的典型案例。自 20 世纪 90 年代初以来，国家积极推动将遗产作为一种“旅游
商品”（Chang 1997），把历史市中心划分为牛车水、甘榜格南和小印度等彼此区分的多种族区域，并围绕由英国
殖民建筑构成的市政中心。这些族群街区按照官方多元种族主义中的“华族—马来族—印度族—其他族群”分类网
格被保存下来。结果，它们成为以族群为主题的街区，在国家打造全球城市的规划中充当旅游景点（Chang 2000；
Yeoh 2005）。
Appiah (2009) locates the problem in the concept of “cultural patrimony” being tied to the state, which exercises power that  
destroys, physically or culturally, heritage objects that should properly belong to humanity. This problem with cultural  
patrimony takes on a pernicious twist in Malaysia as the nation-state’s cultural policies have increasingly highlighted an  
idealised and indigenised Malay past centred on the glories and legends of pre-colonial Malacca (Cartier 1996; Worden  
2010). With Malay heritage emphasised as cultural patrimony, Chinese and Indian cultural practices and buildings are  
denied the status of national cultural property and can be forcibly circumscribed and destroyed. Non-Malay or trans-ethnic  
histories are then easily erased from official discourse and policy, which emphasise Malay primacy in culture and politics  
(Mandal 2004; Ting 2009). Furthermore, the cultural policy is aligned to the bumiputera policy of promoting Malay socio-
economic development through affirmative action in education and business.
Appiah（2009）将问题归结于“文化财产”（cultural patrimony）这一概念与国家相绑定：国家运用权力，在物质
或文化层面摧毁那些本应属于全人类的遗产对象。在马来西亚，这一文化 财产问题呈现出更加有害的变化，因为
民族国家的文化政策日益突出一个被理想化、本土化的马来过去，并以殖民前马六甲的辉煌与传说为中心（Cartier 
1996；Worden 2010）。当马来遗产被强调为国家文化财产时，华人和印度人的文化实践与建筑便被排除在国家文
化财产之外，并可 能受到强制限制甚 至毁坏。非马来人或跨族群的历史也 因此很容易 从官方话语与政策中被抹

[READING EDITION PAGE 4]
去，而这些话语与政策强调马来人在文化和政治上的优先地位（Mandal 2004；Ting 2009）。此外，这一文化政策
还与土著（bumiputera）政策相配合，通过教育和商业领域的平权措施促进马来人的社会经济发展。
The second approach is decidedly more ambivalent. Lowenthal’s (1996: xi) classic argument is that while sharing the same  
field of the past, history “explores and explains pasts grown ever more opaque over time” but heritage “clarifies pasts so as  
to infuse them with present purposes”. In this sense, heritage is more crucial to social life, as it plays an “essential role in  
husbanding community, identity, continuity, indeed history itself.” For Lowenthal, heritage modifies history for the singular  
purpose of undergirding group identity rooted in exclusive legends and myths, drawing selectively and imprecisely from  
distant and generalised history to make for an intimate conflation of past with present (Lowenthal 1996: 139).
第二种路径则明显更为矛盾和复杂。Lowenthal（1996: xi）的经典论点是：历史与遗产虽然都以过去为对象，但历
史“探索并解释那些随着时间推移而愈发晦暗的过去”，而遗产则“澄清过去，使其服务于当下的目的”。从这
个意义上说，遗产对于社会生活更为关键，因为它在维系“共同体、身份认同、连续性，乃至历史本身”方面发
挥着“不可或缺的作用”。对 Lowenthal 而言，遗产为了支撑建立在排他性传说和神话之上的群体身份认同而改造
历 史 ，它 有 选 择地 、 并 不精 确地 从遥 远且 被概 括化 的 历 史 中 取材 ， 从 而让 过 去 与 现 在 紧 密 地 合 并 在 一 起
（Lowenthal 1996: 139）。
Application of the second approach as heritage studies emphasises the process of interpretation. For Howard (2003: 29–30),  
the constructed character of heritage is embraced and the student “proceeds to accept the challenge to manufacture an  
identity, to create heritage, but with an overt agenda and a transparent policy.” The twin problems of market and power are  
acknowledged and they are to be managed in a dialogical manner with interpretation (Howard 2003: 187). In practical  
terms, interpretation is not so much about “bringing the past to life” but “trying to engage with our visitors in the present”  
(Hems 2006: 2), that is, a methodological problem rather than a historiographical or philosophical one.
作为遗产研究，第二种路径的应用强调“阐释”这一过程。Howard（2003: 29–30）接受遗产的建构性特征，认为
研究者应“接受制造一种身份、创造遗产这一挑战，但要具有公开的议程和透明的政策”。市场与权力这两个问
题被承认，并应通过 阐释以对话方式加以 处理（Howard 2003: 187）。从实践层面看，阐释与其说是 “让过去复
活”，不如说是“尝试在当下与我们的参观者建立联系”（Hems 2006: 2）；换言之，它更多是一个方法问题，而
非史学或哲学问题。
Should heritage producers and managers focus on individual sites or should they weave them together in the interpretation  
of the whole town and its urbanism, for example by using trails and walks (Goodey 2006)? How should one approach the  
interpretation of lost ways of life such as industrial heritage as the city redevelops (Price 2006) or adaptively reuse buildings  
that used to house such heritage such as warehouses and factories in a manner sensitive to community participation and  
memory (Moore 2007)? Concretely for the developing countries of Southeast Asia, Henderson (2009) argues that heritage is 
not only cultural capital handed down from generation to generation, but is also social capital binding people together as  
well as political capital for nation building and economic development.
遗产生产者和管理者应当聚焦于单个遗址，还是应当通过步 道和徒步线路等方式，把这些遗址编织进对整座城镇
及其城市形态的整体阐释之中（Goodey 2006）？当城市进行再开发时，应 如何阐释已经消失的生活方式，例如工
业遗产（Price 2006）？又应如何对曾经承载这些遗产的仓库和工厂等建筑进行适应性再利用，同时充分顾及社区
参与和记忆（Moore 2007）？具体到东南亚发展中国家，Henderson（2009）认为，遗产不仅是代际传承的文化资
本，也是把人们联结在一起的社会资本，同时还是国家建构和经济发展的政治资本。
The successful application of George Town and Malacca for UNESCO listing has sparked recent revision of the early  
critical view of heritage production in Malaysia. Instead of seeing heritage production as beholden to the interests of a pro-
Malay state promoting a pro-Malay market, scholars have emphasised the capacity of civil society to promote multicultural  
interests and compacts through local interpretations of heritage (Bideau and Kilani 2012; Nagata 2010; Perkins 2010). In  
Nagata’s view, it was the polysemic nature of the heritage concept that allowed civil society activists to pull disparate

interests together to “shift the focus of heritage away from historic traditions, invented or otherwise, to more universal  
issues beyond ancient ethnic and religious identity lines” (2010: 109).
乔治市和马六甲成 功申请列入联合国教科文组织遗产名录，促使学界近来重新审视早期对马来西亚遗产生产的批
判性看法。学者不再只是把遗产生产视为服务于一个亲马来人的国家、并推动一个 亲马来人的市场，而是强调公
民社会通过地方性的遗产阐释推动多元文化利益和社会协定的能力（Bideau and Kilani 2012；Nagata 2010；Perkins 
2010）。在 Nagata 看来，正是“遗产”概念 本身具有多义 性，使公民社会行动 者能够把彼此 不同的 利益联合起
来，从而“把遗产的焦点从历史传统——无论这些传统是否被发明——转向超越古老族群与宗教身份界线的更普遍
议题”（2010: 109）。
On the other hand, scholars have remained critical and sceptical of the cosmopolitan interpretation of history in Singapore.  
As Johnson (2009: 189) puts it, international arts are valued over local practices, while “ethnic cultural expression and  
heritage precincts” only assume “real value when proscribed and profitable”, hence Singapore as a “Cultural Capital” in the  
making is “ultimately unsustainable”. Comparing three history and arts museums, Ooi (2010) criticises the state for reverse  
Orientalising the city-state as a cosmopolitan Asian society with benevolent governance, positive work ethic and superior  
moral values to place itself as a cultural capital in the region.
另一方面，学者对于新加坡以世界主义方式解释历史仍保持批判与怀疑。正如 Johnson（2009: 189）所说，国际艺
术的价值高 于本地 实践，而“族群文化表达与遗产街区 ”只有在受到规制并能够带来利润时才获得“真正 的价
值”；因此，将新加坡 打造为“文化之都 ”的过程“最终不可 持续”。 Ooi（2010）比较了三座历史与 艺术博物
馆，并批评国家通过一种反向东方主义，把这个城市国家塑造成一个拥有 仁慈治理、积极工作伦理和优越道德价
值的世界主义亚洲社会，以此将自身定位为区域文化中心。
Switching between critical and interpretive approaches depending on whether state, market or civil society is driving the  
process  of  heritage  production  leads  to  one-dimensional  analyses.  Successful  civil  society  ownership  of  heritage  
interpretation and driving of heritage production does not mean commodification has been moderated or power relations  
democratised. On the contrary, they have been reconfigured and repositioned in the social spatial field. On the other hand,  
capitalist market colonisation and the statist imposition of power may be less effective than dispersed commodification and  
enactment of capitalist power relations through civil society actors.
如果仅仅根据国家、市场或公民社会中的 哪一方主导遗产生产， 就在批判路径和阐释路径之间切换，分析便会变
得单一化。公民社会成 功掌握遗产阐释并推动遗产生产，并不意 味着商品化受到抑制，也不意味着权力关系实现
了民主化。相反，这些关系只是被重新配置并重新定位于社会空间场域之中。另一方面，资本主义市场的殖民以
及国家主义式的 权力强加，甚至可能不如通过公民社会行动 者分散进行的商品化，以及资本主义权力关系的实践
化来得有效。
The two approaches are missing the deeper issue. It is not heritage production per se that is the problem. The problem lies in 
the production of heritage space as a particular species of what Lefebvre calls the production of social space. Conceived as  
such,  empty  space  “is  actually  merely  a  representation  of  space”  (Lefebvre  1991:  190,  italics  in  the  original).  The  
representation permits space to be conceived “as being transformed into ‘lived experience’ by a social ‘subject’” (Lefebvre  
1991: 190), be it the market, state or civil society actors. This is the register in which the two theories of heritage production  
operate. The first, critical approach takes historical spaces as socialised spaces subverted by heritage production involving  
hegemonic ideologies. The second, interpretive approach takes heritage spaces as socialised spaces transformed using  
historical ideologies. Both assume a pre-existing empty space that bears socialisation by historical or cultural subjects.
这两种路径都遗漏了一个更深层的问题。问题并不在遗产生产本身，而在于遗产空间的生产——它是列斐伏尔所谓
社会空间生产的一 种特定形式。 按照这种理解，空的空间 “事实上不过是一种空间表征”（ Lefebvre 1991: 190，
原文斜体） 。这 种表 征使空间 能够被设想为 “由一个社会 ‘主体’转化为 ‘生活经验’ ”（ Lefebvre 1991: 
190），这个主体可以是市场、国家或公民社会行动者。这正是上述两种遗产生产理论运行的层面。第一种批判路
径把历史空间视为已经社会化的空间，而遗产生产通过 霸权意识形态使之发生偏转；第二种阐释路径则把遗产空

[READING EDITION PAGE 6]
间视为已经社会化的空间，并通过历史意识形态 对其进行改造。二者都假定存在一个先在的空空间，等待历史或
文化主体对其进行社会化。
By the production of heritage space, I mean the process of forging interrelationships between cultural things by positioning  
and working them into a physical pattern of relative order and disorder that shapes the practice of social life. The work of  
producing space involves the mutually reinforcing representation of space and representational space, the living weight of  
“imaginary and symbolic elements” in “the history of a people as well as in the history of each individual belonging to that  
people” (Lefebvre 1991: 41), or in short, the cultural memories and psychical habits of a community with regard to their  
lived spaces. Both representation of space and representational space make for a spatial practice to produce urban textures.  
The production of heritage space is a species of this work of producing space, in which the taken-for-granted cultural  
memories and habits of a community’s spatial practice are appropriated into the representation of space. In other words, the  
production of heritage space is the production of space when representational space has been made reflexive and functional  
for the representation of space.
我所谓“遗产空间的生产 ”，是指这样一个过程：通过给文化事物定位并对其进行组织，使它们形成一种具有相
对秩序与无序的物质格 局，由此塑造社会生 活的实践。空间生产的工作 涉及空间 表征与表征空间之间的相互 强
化，也涉及“想象性和象征性要素”在“一个民族的历史以及 属于该民族的每一个个体的历史”中具有的鲜活重
量（Lefebvre 1991: 41）；简而言之，就是一个共同体围绕其生活空间形成的文化记忆与心理习惯。空间表征与表
征空间共同构成空间 实践，并由此生产城市肌理。遗产空间的生产 正是这种空间生产工作的一 种形式：共同体空
间实践中那些被视为理所当然的文化 记忆与习惯，被吸收到空间表征之中。换言之，当表征空间被赋予反思性，
并开始服务于空间表征时，遗产空间的生产便成为一种空间生产。
The two approaches to heritage production specify the differences in the production of heritage space to be explained. As I  
have pointed out, the two approaches have been analysing heritage production on the level of the representation of space  
rather than spatial practice. I find two dimensions of analysis particularly useful. First, the critical approach favours a  
cosmopolitan definition of heritage as cultural property, while the interpretive approach privileges the local and multi-scalar  
interpretations of heritage. Malacca, Penang and Singapore all promote a multicultural version of heritage conservation and  
production, but the difference is whether the heritage space producers imagine this multiculturalism as Asian, pitted against  
the West, or hybridising into cosmopolitan admixtures that is international in scope.
关于遗产生产的两 种路径， 指出了我们 需要解释的遗产空间生产差异。 正如我已经 指出的，这两 种路径一 直是
在“空间表征”而非“空间实践”的层面分析遗产生产。我认为有两个分 析维度尤其有用。第一，批判路径偏向
把遗产界定为世界主义意义 上的文化财产，而阐释路径则更重视地方性和多尺度的遗产解释。马六甲、槟城和新
加坡都推动一种多元文化版本的遗产保护与生产，但差异在于：遗产空间的生产 者究竟把这种多元文化想象成一
种与西方相对立的“亚洲”，还是把它想象成一种不断混合、具有国际范围的世界主义形态。
Second, the critical approach emphasises commodification as the end of heritage production, while the interpretive approach 
highlights identity formation. Contrary to studies critical of the overwhelming dominance of the heritage field by the  
developmental state in Singapore and celebrating the dynamism of civil society-led heritage production in Penang, the  
relationship  between  state  and  civil  society  in  heritage  production  is  actually  more  complex  and  contingent.  What  
differentiates the three is whether the terms of state-civil society politics revolve around producing heritage space for tourist  
commodity development or post-colonial identity formation.
第二，批判路径强调商品化是遗产生产的最终目的，而 阐释路径则突出身份认同的塑造。与 那些批评新加坡发展
型国家对遗产领域具有压倒性支配、同时赞扬槟城由公民社会主导的遗产生产 富有活力的研究不同，国家与公民
社会在遗产生产中的关系其 实更加复杂，也更具情境性。三座城市 真正不同之处，在于国家 —公民社会政治的 议
题究竟围绕为旅游商品开发生产遗产空间，还是围绕后殖民身份认同的塑造展开。
In terms of the imagination of its heritage space as Asian or cosmopolitan, Penang tends towards the latter, while Singapore  
the former, with Malacca in between. Penang and Singapore both treat heritage ambivalently as instrumental for identity  

[READING EDITION PAGE 7]
formation and as commodity to sell the city, while Malacca focuses on the latter. As I argue in the next three sections, post-
colonial politics and the political economy of urban redevelopment, both stemming from the contradictory representational  
spaces of port-city legacy and nationhood between 1930s and 1980s, influenced the varied production of heritage space in  
the three cities from the 1990s onwards.
就遗产空间被想象为 “亚洲的”还是“世界主义的”而言，槟城更偏向后者，新加坡更偏向前者，而马六甲介于
二者之间。槟城和新加坡都以一 种矛盾的方式看待遗产：既把它作为塑造身份认同的工 具，也把它作为推销城市
的商品；马六甲则更侧重后者。正如我将在接下来的三个部分中论证的那样，从 20 世纪 30 年代到 80 年代，港口
城市遗产与民族国家身份所形成的相互矛盾的 表征空间，孕育了后殖民政治与城市再开发的政治经济；而这两 者
又共同影响了 20 世纪 90 年代以来三座城市各不相同的遗产空间生产。
MALACCA: ASIAN/COSMOPOLITAN HERITAGE SPACE AS COMMODITY
马六甲：作为商品的亚洲式／世界主义遗产空间
Malacca is the oldest settlement of the three cities. In the fifteenth century, it was the seat of a Malay sultanate. From the  
city, located at one of the narrowest points of the strategic Straits, the Malacca sultanate controlled a major leg in the  
lucrative spice trade route. It soon attracted Chinese traders, who settled in Bukit Cina (Chinese Hill) in the outskirts of the  
main town. As the city was the key link between the Southeast Asia archipelago and the Indian Ocean trade routes  
dominated by Muslim traders, the sultanate embraced Islam and Malacca became an important base from which the traders  
brought Islam to the region.
马六甲是三座城市中最 古老的聚落。15 世纪时，它是一个马来苏丹国的所在地。马六甲位于具有战略意义的海峡
最狭窄地段之一，马六甲苏丹国从这里控制着利润丰厚的香料贸易路线中的一个重要环节。这里很快吸引了华人
商人，他们定居在主城郊外的三宝山（Bukit Cina，意为“华人山”）。由于马六甲是东南亚群岛与由穆斯林商人
主导的印度洋贸易路线之间的关键连接点，苏丹国接受了伊斯兰教，马六甲也成为 商人向这一地区传播伊斯兰教
的重要基地。
From the sixteenth century to nineteenth century, the Portuguese, Dutch and British conquered Malacca in succession. But  
the basic urban morphology established by the Portuguese was preserved. Colonial government buildings occupied the area  
around St Paul’s Hill, where the destroyed old sultan’s palace and colonial fort once stood, on the left bank of the river  
entrance. The main town, where trading activities took place, was situated on the right bank of the river and divided into  
ethnic quarters. However, by the nineteenth century, despite the segregation of the town into ethnic quarters, the ethnic  
communities, though retaining their communal traditions and identities, had formed relatively strong cross-ethnic business  
and social ties. The long centuries of mixed marriages fostered cultural hybridisation. The Malaccan Chinese had become  
Baba Chinese, the Indians became Jawi-Pekans, and the Portuguese Eurasians (Hussin 2007: 271–90).
从 16 世纪到 19 世纪，葡萄牙人、荷兰人和英国人先后征服马六甲。不过，葡萄牙人建立的基本城市形态被保留下
来。殖民政府建筑集中在河口左岸的圣保罗山一带，那里曾是已被毁的旧苏丹王宫和殖民堡垒所在地。贸易活动
集中的主城区位于河流右岸，并被划分为不同族群的居住区。然而，到 19 世纪，尽管城镇仍按族群分区，各族群
在保留自身共同体传统和身份认同的同时，已经形成了相当 紧密的跨族群商业和社会联系。 数百年的通婚推动了
文化混融。马六甲华人逐渐形成峇峇华人群体，印度人形成 Jawi-Pekan 群体，同时还有葡萄牙欧亚裔群体（Hussin 
2007: 271–90）。
In  1924,  to  commemorate  the  centenary  of  British  rule,  Malacca-based  Europeans  published  The Town  and  Fort of  
Malacca.  The  publication  came  shortly  after  Singapore  residents  published  the  two-volume  One  Hundred  Years  of  
Singapore to commemorate the founding of that town. The latter comprised historical essays written by scholarly officials  
and  professionals.  In  contrast,  Town  and  Fort  was  an  illustrated  guidebook  to  the  town  for  visitors.  The  Singapore 
Europeans were doing history, reflecting on the past looking at the city from a distance, while the Malacca Europeans were  
beginning to do heritage, looking up close at the town searching for the past.

[READING EDITION PAGE 8]
1924 年，为纪 念英国统治一 百周年，居住在马六甲的 欧洲人出版了《马六甲城与 堡垒》 （ The Town and Fort of  
Malacca）。就在此前不久，新加坡居民为纪念新加坡建城，出版了两卷本《新加坡一百年》（One Hundred Years 
of Singapore）。后者由学者型官员和专业人士撰写的历史论文组成。相比之下，《马六甲城与堡垒》则是一本面
向游客、配有插图的城市指南。新加坡的欧洲人在“做历史”：他们从一定距离之外观看城市，并反思过去；而
马六甲的欧洲人则已经开始“做遗产”：他们近距离观察城镇，从中寻找过去。
Town and Fort opened with a historical sketch. The pre-colonial Malay and Chinese traffic was recognised, the Portuguese  
were portrayed as ravaging crusaders, and the Dutch seen as interested only in controlling the Straits and maintaining its  
trade monopoly. Unsurprisingly, the British were narrated as benevolent occupiers who preserved the “quaint, dreamy,  
Dutch-looking old town” (Malacca Centenary Committee 1924: 19). Brief descriptions followed of historical monuments  
such as the Stadthuys, the old fort, the ruins of St Paul’s Church, memorial tablets, and tombstones with Armenian and  
Dutch inscriptions. A series of four articles completed the guidebook. The first narrated St. Francis Xavier and his doings in  
and out of Malacca. The second portrayed Christ Church as exemplary of Dutch architecture in Malacca, with references to  
Portuguese tombstones laid in the floor. The third described the Baba Chinese, emphasising how they have absorbed Malay,  
Indian and modern European cultural elements while preserving their Chinese customs to form a unique community. The  
last article touched on the Portuguese community.
《马六甲城与堡垒》以一段历史概述开篇。书中承认殖民前马来人与华人的贸易往来，把葡萄牙人描绘成进行破
坏的十字军式征服者，而荷兰人则被描述为只关心控制海峡并维持贸易垄断。毫不意外，英国人则被叙述为仁慈
的占领者，他们保存了这座“古雅、梦幻、带有荷兰风貌的旧城”（Malacca Centenary Committee 1924: 19）。随
后，指南简要介绍了多处历史遗迹，包括荷兰红屋（Stadthuys）、旧堡垒、圣保罗教堂遗址、纪念碑铭牌，以及
刻有亚美尼亚文和荷兰文的墓碑。指南最后由四篇文章构成。第一篇讲述圣方济各·沙勿略在马六甲内外的活动；
第二篇把基督堂描绘为马六甲荷兰建筑的典范，并提到铺在地面上的葡萄牙墓碑；第三篇介绍峇峇华人，强调他
们在保留华人习俗的同时，吸收了马来、印度和现代欧洲文化元素，从而形成一个独特共同 体；最后一篇则涉及
葡萄牙人社区。
The heritage enterprise was so popular with visitors that the Malacca Historical Society published a second edition of The  
Town and Fort in 1936, renaming it Historical Guide of Malacca. There were a number of notable additions. A new essay  
on the fall of Malacca to the Portuguese by the foremost scholar of Malay society of the period, R.J. Wilkinson, highlighted  
the mentality and life of the Malaccan Malays in their heyday. Historical monuments in the outlying districts featuring  
Malay forts and granite monoliths were described. A member of the Baba Chinese literati rewrote the essay on the Chinese.  
A short article on Malay mosques concluded the guide. These changes reflected two trends related to the upsurge of  
nationalist politics in the 1930s. First, the British were defining Malaya, with Malacca as the symbolic historical centre, as a  
quintessentially Malay country belonging to the Malays. Second, the Baba Chinese in the three settlements had re-identified  
themselves as Straits Chinese, a hybridised, modernised and progressive community loyal to the British who claimed  
political leadership of the native population.
这一遗产项目在游客中非常受欢迎，以至于马六甲历史学会于 1936 年出版了《马六甲城与堡垒》的第二版，并将
其更名为《马六甲历史指南》（Historical Guide of Malacca）。新版增加了若干值得注意的内容。当时研究马来社
会最重要的学者 R.J. Wilkinson 撰写了一篇关于马六甲被葡萄牙征服的新文章，突出描述马六甲马来人在鼎盛时期
的心态与生活。书中还介绍了郊区的历史遗迹，包括马来堡垒和花岗岩巨石。一位峇峇华人文人重写了关于华人
的文章，指南最后又加入一篇关于马来清真寺的短文。这些变化反映了 20 世纪 30 年代民族主义政治兴起相关的两
种趋势。第一，英国人开始把马来亚界定为一个本 质上属于马来人的马来国家，而马六甲则是 它象征性的历史中
心。第二，三个海峡殖民地中的峇峇华人重新把自己认同为海峡华人——一个经过文化混融、现代化并具有进步性
的群体；他们忠于英国，同时声称自己应当在本地人口中发挥政治领导作用。

Between Malacca and Melaka
从 Malacca 到 Melaka
When the Federation of Malaya became independent in 1957, independence was declared in Malacca. The new national  
compact, forged after the collapse of the Malayan Union and revolt by the communists, cemented the special position of the  
Malays. Non-Malays, making up a substantial minority, supported the promotion of bumiputera economic equality in return  
for full citizenship rights and political equality for non-Malays. The sultans became the constitutional heads of state.  
Malacca was of key symbolic significance because the new nation could claim an ancient historical link to the historically  
important Sultanate. Part of the national myth-making is that Malacca was “where it all began”, in the words of a plaque  
unveiled by former Prime Minister Mahathir Mohamed in 1989, which has been the marketing slogan for official tourist  
promotion campaigns. It was also renamed Melaka, its name in Malay.
1957 年马来亚联合邦独立时，独立 宣言是在马六甲宣布的。马来亚联邦方案瓦解以及共产党叛乱之后形成的新国
家政治契约，巩固了马来人的特殊地位。占相当比例的非马来人支持推动土著（bumiputera）的经济平等，以换取
非马来人完整的公民权和政治平等。各州苏丹成为宪政意义上的国家元首。马六甲具有关键的象征意义， 因为这
个新国家可以借此声称自己与历史上重要的马六甲苏丹国拥有古老的历史联系。国家 神话塑造的一部分，是把马
六甲称为“一切开始的地方”——这是前总理马哈蒂尔·穆罕默德于 1989 年揭幕的一块牌匾上的文字，后来也成为
官方旅游宣传活动的营销口号。城市还被改名为马来语名称“Melaka”。
Worden (2010: 135) argues that Melaka’s multicultural history and heritage landscapes have been “reclaimed to some  
extent by a Malay heritage” through the 1970s and 1980s, reflecting the symbolic reinvention of national identity as  
grounded in Malay culture and aligned with the pro-bumiputera provisions adopted after the Sino-Malay race riots of 1969.  
One thing that did not quite gel with this argument was the fact that the Federal Government approached the Dutch  
Government to help plan the restoration of the Stadthuys and other Dutch buildings in the 1980s. The plan, written by Dutch 
restoration architect Laurens Vis (1982), was subsequently published by the National Museum in Kuala Lumpur. Worden  
(2010: 138–9) explains this as the selective appropriation of Dutch colonialism and the downplaying of Portuguese and  
British colonialisms, because the Dutch had been portrayed in official narratives as a neutral power tolerant of Islam and a  
trading ally of the Malay states.
Worden（2010: 135）认为，在 20 世纪 70 和 80 年代，Melaka 的多元文化历史与遗产景观“在一定程度上被马来遗
产重新收编”，这反映了国家身份认同在象征层面的重新发明：国家身份被奠基于马来文化，并与 1969 年华巫族
群冲突后实施的亲土著政策相一致。不过，有一点与这一论述并不完全吻合：20 世纪 80 年代，联邦政府曾主动联
系荷兰政府，请其协助规划荷兰红屋及其他荷兰建筑的修复。这项方案由荷兰修复建筑师 Laurens Vis（1982）撰
写，随后由吉隆坡国家博物馆出版。Worden（2010: 138–9）把这一现象解释为对荷兰殖民主义的选择性挪用，同
时淡化葡萄牙与英国殖民主义， 因为在官方叙事中，荷兰被塑造为一个对伊斯兰教保持宽容、并与马来诸邦保持
贸易同盟关系的中立力量。
However, there are a few reasons for moderating Worden’s argument. First, the Malay interpretation of history is not new,  
but stems from British colonial discourse on Malacca heritage as evident in the  pre-War heritage  guides.  The same  
discourse also provides the space for hybridising non-Malay cultures in Malacca’s heritage. Second, in his restoration  
proposal for the Dutch buildings, Vis ventured beyond the scope of his commission to recommend, among other spaces, the  
conservation of “the living area, the ‘Chinatown’ of Malacca, which is unique in the Far East because of its beautiful old  
houses of Dutch and Chinese origin” (1982: 65). Vis emphasised the charm of the houses along Jalan Tun Tan Cheng Lock,  
the former Heeren Street housing the Dutch gentry before the successful Straits Chinese merchants took over. This pointed  
to the significance of the renaming of the street after the Chinese founding father of the modern nation-state. Third, the  
housing of Malay exhibits in colonial buildings could be read as an exercise in asserting Malay presence in the heritage  
construction of Melaka in a hybridising manner. This development started in colonial times, as is evident in the 1936 Guide.
不过，有几个理由可以使我们对 Worden 的论点作出一定修正。第一，以马来人为中心解释历史并不是新现象，它
源自英国殖民时期关于马六甲遗产的 话语，这一点在战前的遗产指南中已经十分明显。同一套话语也为把非马来

[READING EDITION PAGE 10]
文化以混融方式纳入马六甲遗产提供了空间。第二，Vis 在关于荷兰建筑的修复方案中超出了原委托范围，除其他
地区外，他还建议保护“马六甲的生活区，即‘Chinatown’，这里因保存着具有荷兰与华人来源的美丽老房子，
在远东具有独特性”（1982: 65）。Vis 特别强调陈祯禄街（Jalan Tun Tan Cheng Lock）的房屋魅力；这条街原为
荷兰绅士居住的 Heeren Street，后来由成功的海峡华商接手。这也体现出把街道改以现代民族国家一位华人建国先
驱命名所具有的意义。第三，在殖民建筑中陈列马来人展品，也可以被理解为一 种以混融方式在 Melaka 的遗产建
构中宣示马来人存在的实践。这一发展其实始于殖民时期，1936 年的《指南》已经显示出这一点。
Furthermore, since the abortive attempts to clear the Chinese cemetery on Bukit Cina for urban development in the 1980s,  
which sparked a major controversy that galvanised the Chinese, community and heritage activists have organised to advance 
the equality of interest and stakeholding of non-Malays in the conservation and production of heritage in and around the  
historic town. That the activists were successful in putting their point across could be seen in the unsuccessful attempt by  
the authorities to put Malacca on the World Heritage list in the late 1990s, which were criticised by UNESCO for neglecting 
minority communities (Worden 2010: 143). Over-development that threatened to transform Malacca into a high-rise and  
mega-mall landscape and land reclamation that destroyed the historic waterfront for development were also reasons for the  
rejection in the late 1990s and for another rejection of an earlier application in the 1980s (Lim and Jorge 2005: 353–4).
此外，20 世纪 80 年代当局曾试图为城市开发 清除三宝山华人墓地，但计划失败并引发重大争议，动员了华人社
群。自此以后，社区和遗产行动 者开始组织起来，争取非马来人在历史城区及其 周边的遗产保护与生产中拥有平
等的利益和利益相关者地位。行动者成功表达其立场，可以从 20 世纪 90 年代末当局申请把马六甲列入世界遗产名
录却未获成功一事看出；联合国教科文组织批评该申请忽视少数族群社区（Worden 2010: 143）。过度开发也构成
被拒的原因：它可能把马六甲改造成高层建筑与大型购物中心遍布的景观，而填海开发还破坏了历史滨水区。这
些问题不仅导致 20 世纪 90 年代末申请失败，也是 20 世纪 80 年代更早一次申请被拒的原因之一（Lim and Jorge 
2005: 353–4）。
The latter indicates that the real underlying cause for the heritage politics in Malacca is rapid economic modernisation  
favouring the growth of a Malay rentier class. Because the urban economy is concentrated on trading, the Malaccan Chinese 
middle-class comprises more merchants and entrepreneurs than professionals. The issue for the Chinese is therefore their  
share in the economic benefits of urban redevelopment policy. The problem was that the development plans for Malacca,  
whether they involved taking over heritage buildings and precincts or new heritage-despoiling developments such as the  
building of golf courses, leisure resorts, waterfront hotels, and theme parks, was geared to reasserting Malay economic  
presence in Malacca and therefore excluded the Chinese. For the Malay ruling elites, especially those running the federal  
government, heritage conservation or not, Malacca should recover its pre-colonial status as the great trading city of Melaka.
后一点说明，马六甲遗产政治 真正的深层原因，是快速经济现代化正在推动一个马来 食利阶层的成长。由于城市
经济集中于贸易，马六甲华人中产阶级中商人和企业家多于专业人士。因此，对华人而言，核心问题是他们能够
从城市再开发政策带来的经济利益中分得多少。问题在于，马六甲的发展规划——无论是接管遗产建筑和街区，还
是兴建高尔夫球场、休闲度假村、滨水酒店和主题公园等破坏遗产的新项目——都旨在重新确立马来人在马六甲经
济中的 存在，因此把华人排除 在外。对马来统治 精英，特 别是掌控联邦政 府的精英而言，无论是否进行遗产 保
护，马六甲都应恢复其殖民前作为伟大贸易城市 Melaka 的地位。
There are Malay elites, particularly in the state and municipal governments, who have been open to working with the non-
Malay communities. Discursively, the imagination of a pre-colonial Melaka provide for this, since the Chinese and Indians  
were already trading extensively and settling in the pre-colonial city. In other words, the great Melaka emporium is more  
Asian than it is exclusively Malay in imagination. This opened up space for the engagement with non-Malays that have  
been centred on harmonising heritage conservation with economic development.
也有一些马来精英，特别是州政府和市政府中的精英，愿意与非马来社群合作。在话语层面，对殖民前 Melaka 的
想象为这种合作提供了可能，因为在殖民前城市中， 华人和印度人已经广泛从事贸易并在当地定居。换言之，在

这种想象中，作为大型商贸中心的 Melaka 与其说是纯粹属于马来人的，不如说更具有“亚洲性”。这为与非马来
人互动打开了空间，而这种互动主要围绕如何协调遗产保护与经济发展展开。
In 1988, the Pacific Asia Travel Association (PATA) Malaysia Chapter, which represented multiracial business interests in  
the tourism sector, with the support of the state government and the Malacca State Development Corporation, requested the  
Association send a task force to advise on “tourism development, potential and future possibilities for Malacca, particularly  
in view of its rich historical and cultural assets” (PATA 1989: 1). Malacca was earlier that year declared an “Historic City”  
and laws were made to protect its heritage. The PATA task force appreciated that the state government was deeply  
committed to achieving official World Heritage listing for Malacca, but it noted that “the visible record of Malacca’s history 
is thin, fragile, and not actually dramatic and not at all comparable to most international ‘historic’ sites” (Ibid.: 18).  
Therefore, instead of focusing on the historic core, the authorities should “think of the entire city as part of a larger  
ensemble” (Ibid.: 23).
1988 年，代表旅游业多种族商业利益的亚太旅游协会（PATA）马来西亚分会，在州政府和马六甲州发展公司的支
持下，请求协会派出一个工作组，就“马六甲的旅游发展、 潜力与未来可能性，特别是考虑到它丰富的历史和文
化资产”提供建议（PATA 1989: 1）。同年早些时候，马六甲被 宣布为“历史城市”，并通过法律保护其遗产。
PATA 工作组认可州政府对于争取马六甲正式列入世界遗产名录具有强烈承诺，但同时指出：“马六甲历史留下的
可见记录 稀少 、脆弱 ，而且 实际上并不 十分具有戏剧 性，与大多 数国际 ‘历史遗 址’根 本无法相比”（ Ibid.: 
18）。因此，当局不应只聚焦历史核心区，而应“把整座城市视为一个更大整体的一部分”（Ibid.: 23）。
The task force highlighted the need to position Malacca in world history: “The European era is perhaps the core of the world 
image, and in the European era, it is the Portuguese achievement that is still the most memorable. In no way does this negate 
the importance of the origins of the Malay language, the spread of Islam and the struggle for freedom. But these are regional 
or national highlights and not of world significance” (PATA 1989: 16). In other words, Malacca should be imagined as both  
an Asian and cosmopolitan city. Significantly, the task force recommended the Malacca River as the “centre of history and  
interpretation” rather than the buildings themselves (Ibid.: 33), thus anchoring the heritage imagination in the city’s traffic  
with the world, as “a centre of trade and commerce” (Ibid.: 50). The task force explicitly prefers “Malacca” as the name for  
“tourism purposes and for world recognition” rather than “Melaka” (Ibid.: 39).
工作组特别强调，需要把马六甲放入世界历史中进行定 位：“欧洲时代或许构成其世界形象的 核心，而在欧洲时
代中，葡萄牙人的成就仍然最令人难忘。这绝不否定马来语起源、伊斯兰教传播以及争取自由斗争的重要性，但
这些是区域或国家层面的亮点，并不具有世界性意义”（PATA 1989: 16）。换言之，马六甲应当同时被想象为一
座亚洲城市和一座世界主义城市。 值得注意的是，工作组建议把马六甲河而不是建筑本身作为“历史与阐释的中
心”（Ibid.: 33），由 此把 遗产想象 锚定在这座城市与世界的往来 上，把它 理解为 “贸易 与商业中心 ”（ Ibid.: 
50 ） 。 工 作 组 还 明 确 指 出 ， 出 于 “ 旅 游 目 的 和 世 界 认 知 ” ， 应 使 用 “ Malacca” 这 一 名 称 ， 而 不
是“Melaka”（Ibid.: 39）。
Developing Malacca for Heritage Tourism
为遗产旅游开发马六甲
Through the 1990s and 2000s, the political contest over urban renewal was between those who sought to profit through  
urban redevelopment using an exclusively Malay imagination of Melaka and those who sought multiracial cooperation to  
develop heritage for tourism using the Asian-cosmopolitan imagination of Malacca. The former is heritage insensitive, but  
not  heritage  blind,  as  they  would  exploit  heritage  themes  and  sites  for  theme  park,  glitzy  hotel  and  shopping  mall  
development. The latter group followed the advice of the PATA task force and has gradually spruced up various heritage  
streets, embarked on tourist attraction projects such as the Jonker Walk night-market to bring nightlife to the city, and  
performed extensive renovation works to turn the River into a tourist Riviera.
在 20 世纪 90 年代和 21 世纪初， 围绕城市更新的政治 争论主要发生在两 类人之间：一 类人以一种排他 的马来式
Melaka 想象推动城市再开发并从中 获利；另一类人则以亚洲—世界主义的 Malacca 想象推动多 种族合作，通过发

[READING EDITION PAGE 12]
展遗产 促进旅游。前 者对遗产并不 敏感，但也并 非看不见遗产， 因为他们会利用遗产主 题和遗址来开发主 题公
园、华丽酒店和购物中心。后者则遵循 PATA 工作组的建议，逐步整修多条遗产街道，推出鸡场街夜市等旅游项
目以为城市增添夜生活，并对马六甲河进行大规模改造，把它塑造成旅游“里维埃拉”。
In January 2008, the municipal council adopted the Conservation Management Plan containing many of the PATA task  
force recommendations, which helped clinch World Heritage listing in July that year. Among other things, the Council  
pledged to “increase the participation of all stakeholders including minorities in the planning and decision making process”  
through the formation of a residents’ committee, the consensus of which members would need to be obtained for any  
conservation measures (Melaka Historical City Council 2008: 76). At another level, the Council promised to form “a smart  
partnership between the public sector, NGOs and tourist operators” to foster strategic cooperation for tourism management  
and development (Ibid.: 90). Four interpretive centres representing the Chinese, Malay, Indians and Eurasian communities  
were  planned  to  “generate  awareness  to  the  quickly  disappearing  heritage”  through  “effective  presentation  to  impart 
messages such as ethnic tolerance and harmony which all began in the state of Melaka” (Ibid.: 81).
2008 年 1 月，市议会通过了包含许多 PATA 工作组建议的《保护管理计划》，这帮助马六甲在当年 7 月成功列入
世界遗产名录。除其他措施外，市议会承诺通过成立居民委员会，“提高包括少数族群在内的所有利益相关者在
规划与决策过程中的参与”，任何保护措施都需要获得委员会成员的共识 （ Melaka Historical City Council 2008:  
76）。在另一个层面，市议会承诺在“公共部门、非政府组织和旅游经营者之间建立智慧伙伴关系”，以促进旅
游管理与发展上的战略合作（Ibid.: 90）。规划还拟设立四个阐释中心，分别代表华人、马来人、印度人和欧亚裔
社群，通过“有效展示”来“提高人们对迅速消失的遗产的认识”，并传达“族群宽容与和谐等信息，而这一切
都始于 Melaka 州”（Ibid.: 81）。
But the multiracial group is not immune to leveraging heritage for large-scale development that is of dubious value and  
authenticity. In 2004, the state government announced the controversial construction of a 110-meter high observation tower  
on St Paul’s Hill as a project to draw half a million tourists to the Malacca River area. The slim flagpole shaped tower with  
a revolving circular deck that moves up and down the centre pole is topped by a kris-like structure. This and other  
developments have drawn strong criticisms from conservationists outside of Malacca. In their pictorial tome celebrating  
local place and community identities, Malacca: Voices from the Street, Penang architect Lim Huck Chin and Portuguese  
architect Fernando Jorge (2005: 354) criticised the local authorities for being oriented towards “short-term commercial  
gains” harmful to “Malacca’s unique historical fabric of architecture, communities and culture.”
不过，多种族合作一方同样可能借助遗产推动规模庞大、价值和真实性都令人怀疑的开发项目。 2004 年，州政府
宣布一个备受争议的计划：在圣保罗山上兴建一座 110 米高的观景塔，以吸引 50 万名游客前往马六甲河一带。这
座细长、形似旗杆的塔中央有一个可上下移动的旋转圆形观景平台，顶部则安装类似马来短剑（kris）的结构。这
一项目和其他开发计划受到马六甲以外保护人士的强烈批评。在一本以图片呈现地方场所与社区身份认同的 著作
《马六甲：街道之声》（Malacca: Voices from the Street）中，槟城建筑师 Lim Huck Chin 与葡萄牙建筑师 Fernando 
Jorge（2005: 354）批评地方当局过于追求“短期商业收益”，从而损害“马六甲独特的建筑、社区与文化历史 肌
理”。
What the conservationists have failed to understand is that the Malaccans do not seek identity formation in the production of 
heritage space, as Malacca already has a fabled position in national, regional and world histories. The city’s identity has  
already been sealed. The problem has long been one of economic revival and the commodity value of heritage for tourism  
now promises that the city could at last leapfrog from being stuck in the bygone era of maritime trade to a post-industrial  
service economy.
保护人士没有理解的一点是，马六甲人在遗产空间生产中并不主要 寻求身份认同的塑造， 因为马六甲在国家、区
域和世界历史中早已拥有传奇性的地位，这座城市的身份认同已经基本定 型。长期以来真正的问题是经济复兴，
而如今遗产作为旅游商品所具有的价值，使这座城市终于有 希望从停滞于昔日海上贸易时代的状态中跃升到后工
业服务经济。

PENANG: PRODUCING COSMOPOLITAN HERITAGE SPACE FOR URBAN REVIVAL
槟城：为城市复兴生产世界主义遗产空间
The British established George Town in the northeast corner of Penang Island in 1786. Commercial shophouses were  
clustered in the town centre near the harbour lined up in a grid-like fashion. The Chinese and Chulias (Tamil Muslims) lived 
and worked in the commercial centre. Government buildings and European residences were located in the north-western  
part of town facing the northern coast. Most Malays and Jawi Pekans lived in the southern part of town and in village  
houses beyond the town. The town took shape largely according to the economic needs of the trading port (Hussin 2007:  
141–155). Thus, the ethnic segregation was more a function of the economic division of labour than due to deliberate policy  
or racial differences. Though functionally separated, the different ethnic groups shared a common interest in seeing the new  
town prosper under British rule. Many Malaccan Baba Chinese and Jawi Pekan traders relocated to George Town. As  
hybridised locals well versed in the trade networks and cultures traversing the Straits, they played important roles as  
interethnic mediators. British traders also kept close relationships with Asians. The town quickly established itself as an  
important port in the Bay of Bengal.
1786 年，英国人在槟岛东北角建立乔治市。商业店屋集中在靠近港口的市中心，并以 网格状排列。华人和 Chulia
人（泰米尔穆斯林）在商业中心居住和工作。政府建筑和欧洲人住宅位于城市西北部，面向北部海岸。大多数马
来人和 Jawi Pekan 人居住在城市南部以及城外的甘榜住宅中。城市的形成 很大程度上服从于贸易港口的经济 需要
（Hussin 2007: 141–155）。因此，这种族群隔离更多是经济劳动分工的结果，而不是有意识政 策或种族差异造成
的。尽管不同族群在功能上彼此分离，他们却共享一个共同利益，即希望这座新城在英国统治下 繁荣起来。许多
来自马六甲的 峇峇华人和 Jawi Pekan 商人迁往乔治市。作为 熟悉海峡贸易网络与跨区域文化的混融本地人，他们
在不同族群之间发挥了重要的中介作用。英国商人也与亚洲人保持密切关系。乔治市很快成为孟加拉湾的重要港
口。
The discovery of tin in Perak and Selangor in the 1840s turned Penang into an intra-Straits gateway to Peninsular Malaya.  
Tin transformed the town. George Town exploded with new Chinese migrants en route to the Peninsula. Laissez faire  
colonial government encouraged the growth of Chinese ‘secret societies’ and clan associations that managed the social and  
economic affairs of the new migrants. In 1857, the Municipal Council of George Town was established. The town grew  
denser. The different ethnic groups pulled together in an ever tighter urban mesh of crisscrossing economic and social  
relationships. It was a powder keg that exploded in 1867 when two Chinese secret societies and their Malay and Indian  
allies rioted over the control of tin mines across the channel and shut down the town.
19 世纪 40 年代，霹雳和雪兰莪发现锡矿，使槟城成为海峡内部通往马来半岛的门户。锡矿改变了这座城市。大量
前往半岛的华人新移民涌入乔治市。实行自由放任的殖民政府鼓励华人“秘密会社”和宗族组织发展，由它们管
理新移民的社会与经济事务。1857 年，乔治市市政委员会成立。城市变得更加密集，不同族 群被越来越紧密地拉
进由纵横交错的经济和社会关系构成的城市网络中。这个局面如同一个火药桶，并于 1867 年爆发：两个华人秘密
会社及其马来人和印度人盟友围绕海峡对岸锡矿的控制权发生骚乱，使整座城市陷入停摆。
1867 was also the year that the Straits Settlements became a Crown Colony ruled under direct supervision from London.  
This gave the three towns greater control of their affairs. The Settlements developed rapidly as regional and global trade  
boomed as the world with the expansion of the European empires and hence international capitalism. The rubber boom  
followed shortly, bringing great wealth to the Penang. Until Port Swettenham was greatly expanded in the interwar years to  
serve the Selangor hinterland and the federal capital Kuala Lumpur, Penang was the preeminent gateway entrepôt of  
Malaya.
1867 年也是海峡殖民地成为由 伦敦直接监督的英国皇家殖民地的一年。这使三个城市在自身事务上获得了更大的
自主权。随着欧洲帝国扩张、国际资本主义随之发展，区 域和全球贸易蓬勃增长，海峡殖民地也迅速发展。不 久
之后橡胶业繁荣，为槟城带来巨额财富。在两次世界大战之间的时期，巴生港的前身 Swettenham 港尚未大规模扩
建以服务雪兰莪腹地和联邦首都吉隆坡之前，槟城一直是马来亚最重要的门户型转口港。

In the Straits Settlements period, Penang became a secondary port to Singapore. However, as Turnbull (2009) points out,  
Penang was not a pale reflection of Singapore but a different society. It was much more Indian in character because of the  
Bay of Bengal trade than the Chinese-dominated Singapore. The British element was also more integrated into local society. 
Socially and culturally, the two towns diverged. Politically, Penang was more liberal than Singapore, as the latter, being the  
seat of colonial government in British Malaya, gradually developed a strong state apparatus to govern the larger and riotous  
Chinese population resident in the town and the disparate Malay states under British control. Penang held on to the previous  
era of laissez faire liberalism. Intellectual and educational activities and political movements thrived in Penang, especially  
with the growth of the non-European gentry and middle class.
在海峡殖民地时期，槟城逐渐成为仅次于新加坡的次级港口。然而，正如 Turnbull（2009）指出的，槟城并不是新
加坡的淡化复制品，而是一个不同的社会。由于与 孟加拉湾的贸易，槟城比以华人为主的新加坡具有更浓厚的印
度色彩；当地英国人也更深入地融入本地社会。 因此，两座城市在社会和文化 层面逐渐分化。在政治上，槟城比
新加坡更自由。新加坡作为英 属马来亚殖民政府所在地，为了治理城中规 模更大、时常发生 骚乱的华人人口，以
及英国 控制下彼此分散的马来 诸邦，逐步建立起强大的国家 机器。槟城则 保留了早期自由 放任式自由主义的传
统。随着非欧洲裔士绅和中产阶级的发展，知识、教育活动和政治运动在槟城蓬勃发展。
After the War, the British dissolved the Straits Settlements and caused Penang to join the Malayan Union in 1946 and the  
Federation of Malaya in 1948. A brief secession movement and another to rejoin Singapore, both led by non-Malay elites,  
failed but they set a tone of ambivalence for Penang’s relationship with the future nation. As the most economically  
dynamic state with the only non-Malay majority population in the Federation, Penang had an independent and rebellious  
streak. To appease the citizens that their local interests would be protected, George Town was granted city status by royal  
charter in 1957, months before Malaya became independent.
战后，英国解散海峡殖民地，使槟城于 1946 年加入马来亚联邦，并于 1948 年加入马来亚联合邦。由非马来精英领
导的一场短暂脱离运动以及另一场重新加入新加坡的 运动都以失败告终，但它们为槟城与未来民族国家之间的关
系定下了一种矛盾基调。槟城是联邦中经济最 具活力的州，也是唯一非马来人口占多数的州，因此形成了强烈的
独立与反叛倾向。为了安抚居民、保证地方利益会得到保护，乔治市于 1957 年通过皇家特许获得城市地位，此时
距离马来亚独立只有数月。
National Development and the Decline of George Town
国家发展与乔治市的衰落
1957 also saw the centenary celebrations of the Municipality of George Town. The City Council decided to prepare and  
publish its history to commemorate the centenary. Published in 1966, Penang, Past and Present, 1786–1963 was the  
citizens’ first attempt at producing heritage space. Unlike Town and Fort, the book was purely a detailed account of the  
citizens’ efforts in building up the city without specific monuments listed and highlighted in separate sections. However, the  
account was filled with local place names and the text augmented by many old and current photographs of city landmarks.  
This created a very concrete historical sense of the city that reflected everyday urban familiarity. The city was now  
animated by the agency of its past citizens laying roads, building precincts, constructing infrastructure, establishing urban  
services, resolving communal conflicts, and so on.
1957 年也是乔治市市政机构成立一百周年。市议会决定编写并出版一部城市史，以纪念这一百周年。1966 年出版
的《槟城：过去与现在，1786–1963》（Penang, Past and Present, 1786–1963），是市民第一次尝试生产遗产空间。
与《马六甲城与堡垒》不同，这本书纯粹细致叙述市民建设城市的努力，并没有把特定纪念物单独列出并用独立
章节加以突出。不过，书中充满地方地名，正文还配有大量城市地标的新 旧照片。这创造出一种非常具体的城市
历史感，反映人们对日常城市空间的熟悉。过去市民修筑道路、建设街区、铺设基础设施、建立城市公共服务、
解决社群冲突等行动，也因此使这座城市在叙述中变得鲜活起来。
The account emphasised three ways to think about the space of Penang: as cosmopolitan rather than Asian, democratic, and  
ambivalently integrated into the colony or nation. First, importance was accorded to individuals for building up the city. The 

[READING EDITION PAGE 15]
narrative, by following the municipal archives closely, inevitably gave pride of place to Europeans in the initial period of  
urbanisation.  Non-European  residents  began  to  appear  midway  through  the  account  to  culminate  in  the  clandestine  
evacuation of Europeans from Penang during the Japanese invasion, which was footnoted as having “gave rise to much  
unfavourable comment amongst the local population” (City Council 1966: 82). However, and significantly, other than  
Chinese individuals, many Europeans were listed among those who suffered under the Japanese. Asian individuals became  
prominent in the post-War account.
这部叙述强调了理解槟城空间的三 种方式：它是世界主义的而 非单纯“亚洲的”，是民主的，同时 又以矛盾的方
式被纳入殖民地或民族国家。 第一，城市建设中的个人作用受到重视。由于叙述紧密追随市政档案，城市化早期
不可避免地把欧洲人置于突出位置。到叙述中段，非欧洲居民开始出现，最后讲到日军入侵时欧洲人秘密撤离槟
城；脚注指出，这一行为 “在本地人口中引起许多负面评论”（City Council 1966: 82）。不过，值得注意的是，
在叙述日本占领时期的受害者时，除华人个体外，也列出了许多欧洲人。战后叙述中，亚洲个体开始占据更突出
的位置。
Second, the evolution of the municipal authority was narrated as a process in democratisation. The account linked the  
setting up of a committee of assessors in the 1820s to the institutionalisation of municipal government in England to expand  
the scope of “the principles of democracy” (City Council 1966: 14). The election of municipal commissioners in 1857 in the 
three towns of the Settlements was recounted as a dismal failure. Only Europeans voted in Singapore, while the election in  
Malacca was void due to the lack of voters. The Chinese in Penang voted for representatives opposed to government, but at  
least they voted and had a political position (Ibid.: 19). The narrative of democratisation climaxed in the last chapter with  
the granting of city status in 1957. Partisan elections to the City Council were appraised positively against detractors “who  
think only in terms of physical improvements rather than the evolution of a responsible local administrative body” (Ibid.:  
96).
第二，市政当局的发展被叙述为一个民主化过程。这部叙述把 19 世纪 20 年代评估委员委员会的建立，与英国市政
政府的制度化联系起来，认为其 扩大了“民主原则”的适用范围（City Council 1966: 14）。1857 年海峡殖民地三
座城市 举行市政 委员选举，被描述为一次惨痛 失败：新加坡 只有欧洲人投票，马六甲则 因无人投票而使选举无
效。槟城华人投票支持反对政府的代表，但至少他们确实投了票，并表达了政治立场（Ibid.: 19）。民主化叙述在
最后一章达到高潮，即乔治市于 1957 年获得城市地位。书中积极评价市议会的政党选举，并反驳那些“只从物质
改善角度思考，而不是从一个负责任的地方行政机构演进角度思考”的批评者（Ibid.: 96）。
Third, the fundamental tension threaded through Penang, Past and Present was not the post-colonial narrative of interracial  
communalism versus multiculturalism. Instead, the account wrote of the tension between the municipal government and the  
colony or nation. This began in the late 1880s when the question was whether to make the boundaries of the municipality  
coextensive with the island. That it was not decided so was praised for preventing the municipal government from becoming 
an oppositional force representing Penang against Singapore (City Council 1966: 24). But the “friction” developed anyhow  
and the account proudly narrated the independent streak that municipal commissioners and citizens exhibited against the  
colonial government (Ibid.: 32, 33, 36, 39, 48). The book warned that “the threat of being taken over by the State  
Government is not to be lightly regarded” in post-colonial times (Ibid.: 95).
第三， 贯穿 《槟城：过 去与现在》的根本张力，并不是后殖民 叙事 中“跨种 族共同 体主义 ”与“多元文化主
义”之间的对立，而是市政府与殖民地或民族国家之间的张力。这一张力在 19 世纪 80 年代末已经出现，当时争论
焦点是是否应使市政边界与整个槟岛范围重合。最终没有这样做，书中对此表示肯定，认为这避免了市政府成为
代表槟城、对抗新加坡的反对力量（City Council 1966: 24）。不过，“摩擦”仍然发展起来，这 部叙述还颇为自
豪地讲述市政委员和市民面 对殖民政府时表现出的独立 性（ Ibid.: 32, 33, 36, 39, 48 ）。该书警告说，在后殖民时
期，“被州政府接管的威胁不能被轻视”（Ibid.: 95）。
In 1969, the federal government suddenly revoked Penang’s free port status. The 1970s and 1980s saw George Town  
decline.  The  federal  government  focused  on  developing  Port  Klang,  the  former  Port  Swettenham,  to  service  the  
development of Kuala Lumpur as Malaysia’s primary city. The federal government directed economic development through  

[READING EDITION PAGE 16]
its control over investment in communication, transport, education and health. The opposition Gerakan Party won the 1969  
elections to become the state government, but it joined the ruling coalition in 1973. A compromise was obtained; Penang  
would not be neglected in the national development program. Penang was rapidly developed into a manufacturing hub for  
electronics and engineering.
1969 年，联邦政府突然取消槟城的自由港地位。20 世纪 70 和 80 年代，乔治市开始衰落。联邦政府把重点放在发
展巴生港——即原来的 Swettenham 港——以服务吉隆坡作为马来西亚首要城市的发展。联邦政府通过控制通信、交
通、教育和医疗等领域的投资来主导经济发展。反对党民政党在 1969 年选举中胜出并成为州政府，但于 1973 年加
入执政联盟。双方达成妥协：槟城不会在国家发展 计划中被忽视。此后，槟城迅速被发展为 电子和工程制造业中
心。
However, the development took place largely in the southern parts of the island away from George Town and in Seberang  
Perai, the mainland enclave of Penang state. Younger residents moved out of the inner city into the suburbs. In 1974, to  
revitalise George Town, the state government launched a spectacular project to build the 65-storey KOMTAR shopping and  
commercial complex. The main tower was completed in 1988, but the project’s fifth and last phase has yet to be launched,  
as of 2011. Hundreds of historical shophouses, schools and temples were demolished and damaged in the project, but it  
failed to revitalise George Town (Perkins 2008: 119–21).
然而，这种发展主要发生在 远离乔治市的槟岛南部，以及槟城州位于大陆一侧的飞地威省。年轻居民离开内城，
迁往郊区。1974 年，为了振兴乔治市，州政府推出一项引人注目的计划，兴建 65 层的光大（KOMTAR）购物与
商业综合体。主塔于 1988 年完工，但截至 2011 年，该项目的第五期也是最后一期仍未启动。项目建设过程中，数
百座历史店屋、学校和寺庙被拆除或破坏，但它最终未能使乔治市复兴（Perkins 2008: 119–21）。
Saving George Town through Heritage
通过遗产拯救乔治市
As Lee, Lim and Nor’Aini (2008) argue, state policy did not affect the conservation of George Town and it was the  
unintended consequences of its economic development policy that saved George Town. Development plans to transform  
forested Penang Hill in 1990 galvanised civil society. Concerns regarding environmental degradation and deforestation were 
combined with discourses on the historical and heritage value of the Hill, especially as “a backdrop to the city”, to argue for  
its conservation (Friends of Penang Hill 1991: 39). The plans were shelved and the activists soon turned their attention to  
George Town. This intensified in 2000, when rent control in George Town was repealed and 60,000 long-time tenants  
began to leave, turning the inner city into a ghost town, while property owners planned to redevelop their shophouses for  
commercial use (Nagata 2010: 105).
正如 Lee、Lim 和 Nor’Aini（2008）所指出的，真正保存乔治市的并不是国家的 保护政策，而是其经济发展政 策
产生的非预期后果。1990 年，一项改造森林覆盖的升旗山（Penang Hill）的发展计划激发了公民社会的行动。 人
们把对环境退化和 森林砍伐的担忧，与关于 升旗山历史及遗产 价值的话语结 合起来，特 别强调它 作为“城市背
景”的作用，以此主张保护升旗山（Friends of Penang Hill 1991: 39）。该计划最终被搁置，行动者很快把注意力转
向乔治市。2000 年以后，这 种行动进一步加 强：乔治市取消租金管制，6 万名长期租户开始搬离，使内城一度如
同鬼城，而房产业主则计划把店屋重新开发用于商业用途（Nagata 2010: 105）。
The Penang Heritage Trust mobilised activists and local communities by holding a series of colloquia on the history of  
Penang in 2001 and 2002. Historian Tan Liok Ee wrote, “I was deeply moved by the sense of pride and palpable excitement  
with which a multiplicity of local voices and narratives claimed their place in the making and writing of the Penang story”  
(2009: 7). For Tan, the diverse voices tell a collective story of cosmopolitan Penang “finding confluences” as well as  
“engaging in contestation” in each historical conjuncture, from mercantile port city to economic integration in the nation-
state  to  its  going  global  again  (2009:  24).  The  Penang  Global  Ethic  Project  was  soon  launched  in  2006,  featuring  
workshops, talks and “The Street of Harmony” heritage walk showcasing the cluster of temples, shrines, mosques and  
churches of different ethnic groups and faiths in the Jalan Masjid Kapitan Keling area. The shared UNESCO World  
Heritage listing with Malacca in 2008 was a natural culmination of the activist efforts.

[READING EDITION PAGE 17]
槟城古迹信托会在 2001 和 2002 年举办了一系列槟城历史座 谈会，从而动 员行动者和地方社区。历史学家 陈剑虹
（Tan Liok Ee）写道：“我深受感动，因为如此多元的本地声音和叙事带着自豪感和明显的兴奋，争取自己在槟
城故事的形成与书写中应有的位置”（2009: 7）。在 Tan 看来，这些多元声音共同讲述了一个世界主义槟城的集
体故事：在每一个历史 节点，它既“寻找汇流”，也“参与争论”，从商业港口城市，到被经济 整合进民族国
家，再到重新 走向全球（2009: 24）。2006 年，槟城全球 伦理项目随即启动，举办工作坊和讲座，并推出 “和谐
街”遗产步行路线，展示甲必丹吉宁清真寺路一带不同族群与信仰的寺庙、神庙、清真寺和教堂群。 2008 年，乔
治市与马六甲共同列入联合国教科文组织世界遗产名录，可以说自然地成为这些行动者长期努力的高潮。
George Town conservationists are however split between those who sought heritage conservation as an anchor for the  
Penang identity that is cosmopolitan, democratic and independent-minded and those who believe that a working partnership  
with the tourist industry is needed to revitalise the city and keep the heritage alive. Nanyang Folk Culture, a group of  
bilingual Chinese artist-activists, describes the former position best in their photography book, PENANGism. The book  
aims to give “the World an Imagination about Penang”, to “increase the awareness of tolerance in cultural differences, the  
faith in democracy, and the mutual co-existence in our society”, by portraying “elements of Penang” they believe “to have  
been vitalising Penang’s uniquely urban attributes” (Nanyang Folk Culture 2011: 9).
不过，乔治市的保护人士内部也存在分歧。一部分人希望把遗产保护作为槟城身份认同的 锚点，强调这种身份是
世界主义的、民主的并 具有独立精神；另一部分人则认为，要振兴城市并让遗产保持生命力，就必须与旅游业建
立实际合作关系。由双语华人艺术行动者组成的“Nanyang Folk Culture”团体，在摄影集《PENANGism》中最鲜
明地表达了前一种立场。这本书希望让“世界对槟城产生一种想象”，通过呈现他们认为“一直为槟城独特城市
特质注入活力”的“槟城元素”，来“提高对文化差异的包容意识、对民主的信念，以及我们社会中的相互共 存
意识”（Nanyang Folk Culture 2011: 9）。
Supported by the state government, the tourist industry has been seeking expansion of its base of operations in the northern  
beaches of Batu Feringgi to cultural tourism in George Town (Kahn 1997). A PATA task force was invited to conduct an  
assessment of Penang tourism in 2002. The members were “surprised and impressed with the extent and depth of the  
heritage, history and culture of Penang” and it “was a major hook upon which to hang future promotional campaigns”, but  
they put up a red flag, “The Task Force detected an apparent aloofness and exclusivity or remoteness on the part of the  
[Penang Heritage Trust]” (PATA 2002: 13, 14).
在州政府支持下，旅游业一直试图把自身经营基础从北部峇都丁宜（Batu Feringgi）的海滩旅游扩展到乔治市的文
化旅游（Kahn 1997）。2002 年，PATA 工作组受邀对槟城旅游业进行评估。成员们“对槟城遗产、历史与文化的
广度和深度感到惊讶并留下深刻印象”，认为这些资源“是未来推广活动可以依托的一个重要卖点”；但他们同
时提出警告：“工作组察觉到［槟城古迹信托会］似乎存在一种疏离、排他或与外界保持距离的态度”（ PATA 
2002: 13, 14）。
The World Heritage listing has shifted attitudes among some activists and also the new opposition Democratic Action Party  
state government, such that a compromise between producing heritage for identity and for tourism is emerging. The listing,  
coveted by the activists as it compels the federal, state and municipal authorities to act to conserve George Town, also  
brings  with  it  an  influx  of  tourists.  In  2009,  conservationists  organised  the  “Economics  of  Heritage  Revitalisation”  
conference to learn from eight other World Heritage cities on collaboration between public authorities, the private sector  
and civil society (Khoo 2010). Khoo Salma Nasution, a veteran conservation activist and local historian who was one of the  
main organisers of the conference, became the new president of the Penang Heritage Trust in 2010. She believes that  
maintaining George Town’s World Heritage status is “a balancing act” between heritage conservation and tourism (Chua  
2010: 14), but one that is necessary to save George Town.
世界遗产 列名改变了一些行动 者以及新 上台的反对党民主行动 党州政府的态度，使一种在“为身份认同生产遗
产”与“为旅游生产遗产 ”之间的妥协逐渐出现。行动者之所以渴望获得世界遗产地位，是因为它迫使联邦、州
和市政当局采取行动保护乔治市；但列名同时也带来了大量游客。 2009 年，保护人士举办“遗产复兴经济学”会

议，向另外八座世界遗产城市学习公共当局、私人部门和公民社会之间如何合作（ Khoo 2010）。资深保护行动者
兼地方历史学家 Khoo Salma Nasution 是会议主要组织者之一，并于 2010 年出任槟城古迹信托会新任主席。她认
为，维持乔治市世界遗产地 位是在遗产保护与旅游之间进行 “一场平衡行动”（Chua 2010: 14），但这种平衡是
拯救乔治市所必需的。
SINGAPORE: GRIDLOCKED HERITAGE SPACE AND THE DEVELOPMENTAL STATE
新加坡：被网格锁定的遗产空间与发展型国家
Three years after Singapore was founded in 1819, Stamford Raffles gave detailed instructions for a town plan with tight  
urban grids. The left bank of the Singapore River, the main artery of commerce, was reserved for commercial buildings. To  
the immediate east was located the civic district and the European town. A plain to the north of the town buffered it from  
the native cantonment, hospital and the Indian washers’ village to the northeast. East of the European town was the palace  
and mosque of Sultan Hussein, and Arab and Bugis kampongs. The Chinese and Indian Muslims were placed in their  
respective kampongs on the right bank of the river.
1819 年新加坡开埠三年后，斯坦福·莱佛士对一项采用严密城市网格的城市规划作出详细指示。作为商业主要动脉
的新加坡河左岸被划为商业建筑区。其正东面是市政区和欧洲人城区。城市北面的一 片平地将其与东北方向的本
地人营区、医院以及印度洗衣工人村隔开。欧洲人城区以东则是苏丹胡先的王宫和清真寺，以及阿拉伯人与武吉
士人的甘榜。华人和印度穆斯林则分别被安置在新加坡河右岸各自的甘榜中。
The resultant rigid racial segregation did not mean that the native residents submitted to its racial logic. As Pieris (2009:  
159–161) shows, the natives developed alternative pluralisms that were expressed in multiple names for streets in different  
languages. Such hybridising vernacular pluralism in Singapore was not different from that found in Malacca and Penang,  
but the spatial expression of the pluralism between the three towns was significantly different. As Pieris (2009: 162, 161)  
puts it, George Town’s natives “claimed their new geography as integral to their efforts at self-determination” while  
Malaccans “produced hybrid rural enclaves”. In other words, hybridising vernacular pluralism found its way into the  
production of space in Penang and Malacca, in the lattices of the streets in the former and in the agglomerations of kampong 
neighbourhoods in the latter. On the other hand, the Singapore residents were only able to subvert the urban grid through  
vernacular naming and micro-spatial struggles over sidewalks (Yeoh 2003).
由此形成的严格种族隔离，并不意味着本地居民完全服从这种种族逻辑。正如 Pieris（2009: 159–161）所示，本地
人发展出了另一种多元主义，它体现在同一条街道可以拥有不同语言的多个名称。新加坡这种具有混融性质的本
土多元主义，与马六甲和槟城并没有本质差异，但三座城市中这种多元主义的空间表达方式却显著不同。用 Pieris
（2009: 162, 161）的话说，乔治市的本地人“把新的地理空间视为其争取自我决定努力的一部分”，而马六甲人
则“生产出混融的乡村飞地”。换言之，在槟城和马六甲， 混融的本土多元主义真正进入了空间生产：前 者体现
为街道格局中的交织网络，后者则体现为甘榜社区的聚集。相比之下，新加坡居民只能通过本土语言命名，以及
围绕人行道展开的微观空间斗争，对城市网格进行有限的颠覆（Yeoh 2003）。
From 1867 onwards, with the establishment of the Straits Settlements and Singapore as the seat of colonial government, the  
urban grid hardened further. The building of new defences, the Government House, and the new prison strengthened the  
urban grid. As Pieris (2009: 213) argues, Singapore was being locked into a prison mode of producing space overlaid with a  
racial urban grid. The rubber boom before the First World War saw the mixing of neighbourhoods as the city centre became  
denser. However, the town expanded with grid logic, with the Chinese area pushing westwards to Tiong Bahru, Malay  
kampongs expanding eastward to Geylang, the Indians pushing northeastwards on Serangoon Road and the Europeans and  
Straits Chinese pushing northwestwards through Orchard Road.
从 1867 年海峡殖民地正式建立、新加坡成为殖民政府所在地以后，城市网格进一步固化。新的防御工事、总督府
和新监狱的建设强化了这一网格。正如 Pieris（2009: 213）所说，新加坡逐渐被锁定在一种“监狱式”的空间生产
模式中，其上又覆盖着种族化城市网格。第一次世界大战前的橡胶繁荣，使市中心在不断增密过程中出现社区混

[READING EDITION PAGE 19]
合。然而，城市 整体仍沿着网格逻辑向外扩展：华人区向西延伸至中峇鲁，马来甘榜向东扩展到芽笼，印度人沿
实龙岗路向东北扩展，而欧洲人和海峡华人则沿乌节路向西北推进。
As Britain’s metropolis in the East, Singapore also became an experimental site for applying modern municipal and urban  
redevelopment  ideas  to  the  colony.  In  1927,  the  Singapore  Improvement  Trust  was  established  to  develop  modern  
sanitation, road infrastructure and public housing, and improve urban design. In 1936, the first public housing estate in  
Tiong Bahru was built, comprising thirty blocks of low-rise walk-up apartments. Construction continued apace after the  
War.
作为英国在东方的都会，新加坡也成为 把现代市政管理和城市再开发理念应用于殖民地的实验场。 1927 年，新加
坡改良信托局（Singapore Improvement Trust）成立，负责发展现代卫生设施、道路基础设施和公共住房，并改善
城市设计。1936 年，中峇鲁建成第一座公共住房住宅区，由 30 栋低层无电梯公寓组成。战后，建设仍快速推进。
As Widodo (2004: 139) observes, the Tiong Bahru estate resembled progressive British New Town design principles:  
“small neighbourhoods”, “maximum privacy between individual homes”, “the need to promote health and to improve  
security through visibility and public surveillance.” The apparent contradiction between maximum individual privacy and  
enhanced public surveillance is lost in Widodo’s positive appraisal. Public housing was the metropolitan welfare state’s  
extension of benevolent paternalism to the colonies, to transform natives into modern citizens who could eventually take up  
the burden of independence. The mixture of socialist and liberal principles was produced spatially to hone the better man.  
The natives would be civilised to adopt cultured public behaviour, while the home would be the private space of self-
cultivation and domestic sociality.
Widodo（2004: 139）指出，中峇鲁住宅区体现了英国进步主义“新城”设计原则，包括“小型社区”、“住宅之
间最大限度的私人空间”，以及“通过可见性与公共监视促进健康并改善安全的需要”。在 Widodo 的正面评价
中，“最大限度的个人隐私”与“强化公共监视”之间显而易见的矛盾被忽略了。公共住房是大都会福利国家把
仁慈的家长主义延伸到殖民地的方式，其目的在于 把本地人塑造成现代公民，使他们最终能够承担独立的重任。
社会主义与自由主义 原则的混合通过空间形式被生产出来，以 磨炼“更好的人”。本地人将在公共空间中被文明
化，学会有教养的公共行为；而家庭则成为自我修养和家庭社交的私人空间。
Post-colonialism Gridlocked
被网格锁定的后殖民主义
Soon after it was elected into power in 1959, the People’s Action Party government began to clear inner-city slums and  
move citizens into mass-constructed flats. Public housing construction accelerated with a vengeance after independence. In  
1965, 23 per cent of the population lived in public housing flats. By 1990, the figure reached 87 per cent (HDB Annual  
Report 2007/2008: 60). Housing estates were located in two axial extensions westwards to the factories in Jurong and  
eastwards to the Airport, and in a ring surrounding the central catchment reservoirs. Citizens were encouraged to buy their  
flats, thereby creating “a property-owning democracy” (Kong and Yeoh 2003: 96), in which the citizens would be strongly  
committed to neighbourhood, society and country.
人民行动党政府于 1959 年上台后不久，便开始清理内城贫民区，把居民迁入大规模兴建的组屋。独立以后，公共
住房建设进一步急剧加速。1965 年，有 23%的人口居住在公共住房中；到 1990 年，这一 比例已达到 87%（HDB 
Annual Report 2007/2008: 60）。住宅区沿两条轴线向外延伸，一条向西通往裕廊工厂区，另一条向东通往机场，
同时还在中 央集水区水库周围形成一 圈。政府鼓励居民购买自己的组屋，从而 建立一种“有产者民主”（Kong 
and Yeoh 2003: 96）；在这种体制中，居民会对邻里、社会和国家形成强烈的投入和归属。
Public housing was geared to the virtuous cycles of economic development, heritage evolution and nation building. As the  
Chief Executive Officer of the Housing and Development Board wrote in 1982, in Our Heritage and Beyond, “public  
housing estates … once equipped with the essential facilities, should provide maximum flexibility for people to evolve their  
own life-styles. Political stability and successful economic development schemes make it possible to upgrade physical  
environment in the housing estates. This in turn must upgrade the quality of life, reinforce political stability and facilitate  

[READING EDITION PAGE 20]
economic  development”  (Liu  1982:  133).  To  influence  the  evolution  of  lifestyles  and  discipline  public  culture,  the  
government used the racial logic encoded in the urban grid defining the inner city and mapped it unto the new urban spaces  
of the public housing estates. Community centres were established in every housing estate and government-appointed  
grassroots  leaders  organised  language  classes,  cultural  performances  and  other  ethnic-themed  events  encouraging  
multicultural interaction. The hybridisation encouraged was not vernacular and organic, but mechanically structured in the  
racial grid of Chinese, Malay, Indian, and Others.
公共住房被纳入经济发展、遗产演进和国家建构之间的良性循环。正如建屋发展局首席执行官 1982 年在《我们的
遗产及其未来》（Our Heritage and Beyond）中所写：“公共住房住宅区……一旦配备了基本设施，就应当为人们
发展自己的生活方式提供最大灵活性。政治稳定和成功的经济发展计划使改善住宅区物质环境成为可能，而这又
必须进一步提升生活质量、强化政治稳定并促进经济发展”（Liu 1982: 133）。为了影响生活方式的演变并规训公
共文化，政府把定义内城的城市网格中所编码的种族逻辑，映射到新的公共住房城市空间之上。每个住宅区都设
立社区中心，由政 府任命的基层领袖组织语言课程、文化表演以及其他以族群为主题的活动，以鼓励多元文化互
动。这里所鼓励的混融并非本土自发、自然形成的，而是 机械地按照“华人、马来人、印度人和其他族群”的种
族网格加以组织。
Intellectuals gave substance to the grid. In 1975, a symposium on cultural heritage featured talks by four prominent  
intellectuals on the heritage of the four races. V.T. Arasu, editor of the Tamil newspaper Tamil Murasu, spoke on Hindu  
culture. He noted that urban renewal and the resettlement of Indians had created “problems for the established temples  
operating on old patterns” and focused on rituals, but was hopeful that some were modernising by offering religious  
education and promoting dance, music and literature. Plans, he announced, were afoot to build “multi-purpose temples in  
new towns” to “cater to the artistic, cultural and social needs as well” (Arasu 1975: 15).
知识分子为这一网格提供了实质内容。1975 年，一场关于文化遗产的研 讨会邀请四位重要知识分子分别讨论四个
种族的遗产。《淡米尔之声》（Tamil Murasu）编辑 V.T. Arasu 讨论印度教文化。他指出，城市更新和印度人的重
新安置已经给“按照旧有模式运行的既有寺庙”带来“问题”，使它们过度集中于仪式；但他也乐观地看到，一
些寺庙正通过开展宗教教育并推广舞蹈、音乐和文学实现现代化。他还宣布，已经在规划于新镇建设“多功能寺
庙”，以“同时满足艺术、文化与社会需要”（Arasu 1975: 15）。
Arasu’s criticism of the old patterns of Hindu culture represented the post-colonial government’s approach to the production 
of heritage space. Temples, churches, and mosques were allocated space in the new towns to serve functions beyond  
religious practices. They would not only preserve the intangible heritage of the major racial groups in the new urban space,  
they would also help modernise the people. The government-led grassroots movement based in the community centres  
would then bring these modernising heritages together to facilitate the building of a multiracial nation. On the national level, 
the Ministry of Culture took on the task of synthesising the heritage development by producing encyclopaedias. Other than  
cultural practices, beliefs and practices, the Ministry’s The Singapore Heritage magazine, published in the 1980s, featured  
streets in the downtown district with historical architecture, thereby cultivating a nascent heritage consciousness that harked  
back to the city centre for tangible heritage while promoting intangible heritage practices in the new towns.
Arasu 对印度教文化旧有模式的批评，体现了后殖民政府生产遗产空间的方式。新镇为寺庙、教堂和清真寺划出专
门空间，使其承担超越宗教实践本身的功能。这些宗教场所不仅要在新的城市空间中 保存各主要种族群体的非物
质遗产，还要 帮助民众实现现 代化。以社区中心为基础、由政 府主导的基 层运 动随后 把这些 “现 代化中的遗
产”聚合起来，以促进多种族国家的建构。在国家层面，文化部承担起整合遗产发展的任务，并通过编纂百科全
书来完成这一工作。除了文化实践和信仰等内容，文化部在 20 世纪 80 年代出版的《新加坡遗产》（The Singapore 
Heritage）杂志还介绍市中心具有历史建筑的街道，由此培养一种初步的遗产意识：一方面 回望市中心的 物质遗
产，另一方面在新镇中推广非物质遗产实践。

At the Margins of the Gridlock
在网格困局的边缘
Urban redevelopment was ramped up further in the 1990s to transform the entire downtown area that was formerly  
gridlocked by Raffles’ town plan into the global city centre of Singapore. Plans were made to preserve the educational  
buildings and old governmental buildings of monumental scale in the area for adaptive re-use, meaning to convert them into  
commercial hotspots, and museums and art galleries managed by the newly formed National Heritage Board under the  
Ministry of Culture. The underlying objective of the transformation was to produce monumental heritage spaces that would  
open up the urban communes and their thick cultural and social networks to global capital, migration and tourism flows.
进入 20 世纪 90 年代，城市再开发进一步加速，目标是把原本被莱佛士城市规划“网格锁定”的整个市中心改造为
新加坡的全球城市中心。政 府计划保护该地区具有纪念性尺度的教育建筑和旧政府建筑，并进行适应性再利用，
即把它们改造成商业热点，以及由文化部下新成立的国家文 物局管理的博物馆和艺术馆。转型背后的根本目标，
是生产 具有纪念性的遗产空间，从而 把城市共同 体及其浓密的文化和社会 网络向全球 资本、移民与旅游 流动开
放。
Earlier plans were made to conserve Kampong Glam, the area around the Sultan Mosque, as the Malay district, Little India,  
and Chinatown as the Asian counterparts to the civic district. They were now to be integrated into the global city urban plan  
that would anchor Singapore’s Asian national identity in tangible heritage, so that the citizens’ heightened sense of nostalgia 
in the face of rapid development and dislocation could find a historical orientation in the old city (Yeoh and Kong 1996). At  
the same time, the preserved tangible heritage would serve as the foundational lattice for urban redevelopment of the  
gridlocked old city districts into ethnic-themed tourism precincts.
更早的规划已经提出保护甘榜格南——以苏丹清真寺为中心的马来人街区——以及小印度和牛车水，把它们作为与
市政区相对应的“亚洲”空间。如今，这些街区将被纳入全球城市规 划，通过有形遗产 锚定新加坡的亚洲国家身
份，使居民在快速发展和迁移造成的强烈怀旧情绪中，可以从旧城找到一种历史定位（Yeoh and Kong 1996）。与
此同时，被保存的有形遗产又将成为基础网架，支撑那些被网格锁定的旧城区进行城市再开发，并转化为以族 群
为主题的旅游街区。
In 1998, the Singapore Tourism Board announced a grand project to remake Chinatown with colour-coded neighbourhoods,  
new “elemental” gardens and a “Village Theatre-Temple complex” that would be “recalling and revitalising the Chinatown  
spirit so fondly remembered by Singaporeans and visitors alike” (quoted in Kwok et al. 2000: 6). Activists reacted with  
criticisms that were furious by the standards of a country with a softening authoritarian regime. The Singapore Heritage  
Society championed the public cause and reminded the developmental state that Chinatown still possessed “a heritage –  
living traditions and communal spaces that continue and change up to the present day” (Kwok et al. 2000: 17). Instead of  
“trying to capture everything Chinese” in the village theatre and temple complex, the Society called for the Board to “let the  
population and resident groups decide what should be included in this communal building”, so that tourists would be  
encouraged “to mingle with the local crowd and observe the living patterns of the Chinatown population” (Ibid.: 22). It also  
emphasised that the elemental garden idea was “grossly inappropriate and ignorant” because Chinatown “is multi-ethnic”,  
“not a minority racial enclave” and “therefore, also not a destination of exotic oriental culture” (Ibid.: 22).
1998 年，新加坡旅游局宣布一项大规模改造牛车水的计划，包括以颜色区分的社区、新的“元素”花园，以及一
个“村庄剧场—寺庙综合体”，其目标是“重新唤起并振兴新加坡人和游客如此怀念的牛车水精神”（引自 Kwok 
et al. 2000: 6）。在一个威权体制正逐渐软化的国家中，行动者的批评按当地标准可谓相当激烈。新加坡古迹学会
代表公众利益发声，并提醒发展型国家：牛车水仍然拥有“一种遗产——一直延续到今天并不断变化的生活传统和
公共空间”（Kwok et al. 2000: 17）。学会反对在村庄剧场—寺庙综合体中“试图包罗一切中国元素”，而要求旅
游局“让居民和居民团体决定这座公共建筑中应包含什么”，从而鼓励游客“融入本地人群，并观察牛车水居民
的生活模式”（Ibid.: 22）。学会还 强调，“元素花园”的构想“极其不恰当且无知”，因为牛车水“ 是多族群
的”，“不是少数种族飞地”，因此“也不是一个展示异国东方文化的目的地”（Ibid.: 22）。

[READING EDITION PAGE 22]
Significantly, the Society reminded the Board that many residents had relocated from shophouses into public housing flats  
in the area and that the residents had maintained their social life, commercial relationships and cultural practices in the  
town. They thus provide “precisely the types of cultural resources and experiences” Singaporeans could share with visitors  
(Kwok et al. 2000: 20). Elemental gardens and the village theatre and temple complex were scrapped, but the Chinatown  
residents remained segregated from the spruced up tourist zones. One could walk from a tourist street selling ‘heritage’ food  
and fashion to a residential centre a stone throw’s away to get the same items at half the price. The re-articulation of the  
community’s intangible heritage with the tangible heritage of place that the Society had called for ran against the history of  
racially gridlocked urbanism.
值得注意的是，学会提醒旅游局，许多居民虽然已经从店屋搬入该地区的公共 住房，但仍然在这里维持他们的社
会 生活 、 商 业 关 系 和 文 化实 践。 因 此， 他 们 恰 恰提 供 了 新 加 坡人 能够 与 游客 分 享 的 “ 那 种文 化资 源 与 经
验”（Kwok et al. 2000: 20）。最终，“元素花园”和村庄剧场—寺庙综合体被取消，但牛车水居民依旧与整修一
新的旅游区相互 隔离。人们可以从售卖“遗产”食品和时尚商品的旅游街步行到 几步之遥的居民中心，以一半价
格买到同样的东西。古迹学会所主张的、把社区非物质遗产重新与地方有形遗产 连接起来的做法，与长期以来种
族化网格城市主义的历史发生了冲突。
The gridlock extended into the 2000s. Heritage activists made little progress conserving living heritage spaces, except in  
cases where their conservation targets aligned with the urban planning objectives of the developmental state. Therefore,  
architects and Peranakan Chinese campaigning for the conservation of the old towns of Tiong Bahru and Joo Chiat  
respectively have met with some success. However, success brought with it gentrification, such that the quaint old world  
lifestyles activists had sought to preserve gave way to hipster cafes and well-heeled residents. On the other hand, long-time  
residents of old public housing estates in Queenstown and Rochor have been resettled for urban redevelopment, despite  
unpublicised protests and discontent.
这种网格困局延续到 21 世纪初。遗产行动者在保存“活态遗产空间”方面进展有限，只有当他们的保护目标与发
展型国家的城市规划目标一致时，才可能取得成效。因此，分别推动保护中峇鲁和如切老社区的建筑师与土生华
人团体取得了一定成功。然而，成功也带来了绅士化：行动者原本希望保存的古雅旧式生活方式，逐渐让位于潮
流咖啡馆和富裕居民。另一方面，女皇镇和梧槽等老公共住房区的长期居民即使存在未公开的抗议和不满，仍因
城市再开发而被重新安置。
A large part of the power of the state stems from its ability to control historical information, thereby limiting the ability of  
citizen activists and historians to produce their own heritage space (Dobbs 2010). Interestingly, the conservation movement  
that had galvanised citizens in recent years is focused on Bukit Brown Cemetery, the remaining public cemetery in  
Singapore where many colonial period luminaries are buried. The movement has seen intensive efforts in producing  
historical knowledge, turning the previously little-known cemetery into a veritable archive. Activists have also transformed  
it into a new vernacular heritage space through online publications and public talks and walks, thus forcing the government  
into engaging with the activists, off the grid.
国家 权力很大一 部分来自其 控制历史 信息 的能力，这 限制 了公民行动 者和历史学家生产自身遗产空间的 能力
（Dobbs 2010）。有意思的是，近年来真正动员大量市民的保护运动，集中在武吉布朗坟场——这是新加坡现存的
公共墓地，许多殖民时期的重要 人物埋葬于此。运动推动了密集的历史 知识生产，使这个此前鲜为人知的墓地变
成一座真正意义上的档案库。行动者还通过网络出版物、公共讲座和步行导览，把这里转变为一个新的本 土遗产
空间，并由此迫使政府在既有“网格”之外与行动者展开互动。
CONCLUSION
结论
History is not destiny, but the production of heritage space reinstitutes the dominant cultural logic of the past encoded in the  
urban fabric of the city in the material present, so that history defines destiny. The colonial urbanisms of the three British  
port cities have strongly influenced the way each city-state approached its inherited urban space in the contemporary period  

[READING EDITION PAGE 23]
to deal with the exigencies of post-colonial identity and economic development. When colonial urbanism was highly  
segregationist, the post-colonial civic leaders tended to adopt an Asian orientation to imagine the city. When colonial  
urbanism facilitated cultural hybridity, the civic leaders tended to adopt a cosmopolitan imagination. Singapore and Penang  
form two ends of this spectrum from segregation to hybridity, with Malacca in between.
历史并非命运，但遗产空间的生产会 把过去编码在城市肌理中的主导文化 逻辑重新植入当下的物质现实之中，于
是历史开始界定命运。三个英国港口城市的殖民城市主义， 深刻影响了各城市国家在当代 如何处理继承下来的城
市空间，以回应后殖民身份认同和经济发展的 迫切要求。当殖民城市主义 具有高度隔离性时，后殖民时期的城市
领导者往往采用亚洲取向来想象城市；当殖民城市主义 促进文化混融时，他们则更倾向采用世界主义想象。新加
坡与槟城分别位于从隔离到混融这一光谱的两端，马六甲则处于二者之间。
The post-colonial exigencies of nationalism and developmentalism mattered for all three city-states, but in different ways.  
Problematic engagements with nationalism focused heritage production on identity, while local developmentalism would  
compel the treatment of heritage as commodity. Assured of its historical identity and already the symbolic centre of the  
nation, Malacca is seeking to leapfrog into the contemporary post-industrial age on the back of cultural tourists washed  
ashore by globalisation. Penang, proudly cosmopolitan and democratic, but deeply ambivalent about its relationship with  
the nation, has to balance identity assertion and heritage tourism in order to save George Town from crumbling after  
decades of economic neglect. Singapore is still gridlocked by its colonial racial urbanism, as though Raffles’ town plan is  
still in use, its citizens boxed into concrete flats laid out in new racial grids of intangible heritage, while the state faces the  
contradiction between multiracial national identity making and tourism-oriented urban redevelopment.
民族主义和发展主义 带来的后殖民压力，对三个城市国家都 很重要，但作用方式 各不相同。与民族主义之间 存在
问题的关系，会使遗产生产更集中于身份认同；而地方发展主义则会 迫使城市把遗产作为商品来处理。马六甲对
自身历史身份相当 确定，而且已经是国家的象征 性中心，因此希望借助被全球化浪潮带来的文化游客，一跃进入
当代后工业时代。槟城以世界主义和民主传统为 荣，却对自身与民族国家之间的关系 始终抱有深刻矛盾，因此必
须在身份认同表达和遗产旅游之间 保持平衡，才能把经历数十年经济忽视后逐渐衰败的乔治市挽救下来。新加坡
则仍被其殖民时期的 种族城市主义所“网格锁定”，仿佛莱佛士的城市规划仍在继续运行：居民被装入按照新的
非物质遗产种族网格排列的混凝土组屋中，而国家则面 对多种族国家身份塑造与面向旅游的城市再开发之间的矛
盾。
Heritage does not reflect the evils of capitalism or state power, nor does it reflect the natural or constructed identity of  
communities. Heritage is a symptom of the production of space in an era when capitalism, the state, and identity formation  
have become closely intertwined. Heritage and urban studies scholars have been arguing over the questions of what are  
heritage and the Asian city separately. The time is ripe to consider the two questions as stemming from the same theoretical  
problem. This is the problem of specifying the historicity of the post-colonial city in globalising Asia, just as the civic  
leaders have become reflexive and active in narrating their city’s history and turning it into heritage.
遗产既不是资本主义之恶或国家权力的直接反映，也不是共同体自然身份或建构身份的直接反映。遗产是空间生
产的一种症候；在这个时代， 资本主义、国家与身份认同塑造已经 紧密交织在一起。遗产研究和城市研究的学 者
一直分别争论“什么是遗产”和“什么是亚洲城市”这两个问题。现在应当把二者视为源自同一个理论 问题。这
个问题，就是如何具体说明全球化亚洲中后殖民城市的历史 性；与此同时，城市领导者也正变得更具反思性，更
积极地主动讲述城市历史，并把它转化为遗产。
The post-colonial city is finally coming of age and experimenting with urban forms to find its place in the world. We need  
to match its reflexivity without being caught up with its heritage production. We need to understand the historicity of the  
production of urban space, not just criticise or valorise the heritage constructions that are part of the production, as the  
issues run deeper than the apparent meanings and sentiments that the people are fighting over. It is nothing less than the  
spirits of capitalism that the people are seeking to domesticate in their material lifeworld.

后殖民城市终于 逐渐成熟，并开始尝试不同的城市形式，以 寻找自身在世界中的 位置。我们需要以同等的反思能
力回应它，而不能被其遗产生产本身所 裹挟。我们需要理解城市空间生产的历史 性，而不能只批判或赞美作为空
间生产一部分的遗产建构，因为真正的问题比人们争论的那些表面意义和情感更深。人们试图在自己的物质生活
世界中加以驯化的，不少于资本主义的“精神”本身。
